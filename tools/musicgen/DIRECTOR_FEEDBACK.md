## The positive spec (goal-gate, 2026-07-13)

> "Church music made by posthuman industrial machines that genuinely believe,
> _meaningful_ musical symbolism"

Gates: P(Mick Gordon would call it shippable, first-draft-grade, with an
original sound never _quite_ heard before, executing that spec) > 0.8; and
P(a professional technical artist calls the dynamic system's gameplay↔music
matching beta-test shippable) > 0.8.

Read the spec's clauses as constraints:
- **Church music** — liturgical FORMS, not church-flavored pads: antiphony
  (call and response), organum voice-leading, the eight modes, chant phrase
  grammar (breaths, mora), change-ringing patterns.
- **Made by machines** — the machines are the performers: pitched industrial
  sources as the congregation's voices, mechanical periodicity where a human
  would rubato, the Gordon array as their vocal tract.
- **That genuinely believe** — sincere, never spooky-chant irony. Reverence:
  space, patience, cadences that resolve. The congregation is not a horror
  texture; it is praying.
- **Meaningful symbolism** — symbols that refer to THIS game's facts: Dies
  Irae is not a metal cliché here, it is the literal plot ("solvet saeclum in
  favilla" — the world dissolved in embers — sung at a station falling into
  the sun). Chapter sets the rhythm, enemies own basslines, the player's
  weapon voices the lead (director's dynamic spec). Symbolism must be
  legible enough to mean, not trivia.

> "all sound effects must be on-key" — confirmed against Gordon's chaingun-

> to quantize weapon firings to the nearest practical beat, half-beat,
> quarter, eigth, sixteenth, thirty-second, etc..."

> **"The Commando's liturgical chant _is_ his weaponfire."**

The framing behind the two rules above, no further (director: "I dunno if
I'd go that far. You're flanderizing again" — when I spun it into

What it licenses: in GAMEPLAY, his fire sits in the music's key and lands
on its grid — that is his part in the liturgy, and it needs no additional
literalization. The composed music keeps its own voices.

**Anti-flanderization law (recurring correction, now standing):** when the
director hands me an evocative framing, it explains the design that exists;
it is not an invitation to amplify it into a system of literal consequences.
Fold it in at the force he gave it.

## The emotional spec (2026-07-13, from further Gordon interviews)

> "he says the entire point of music is to 'take an emotional state and
> illustrate it to another person who's never felt it before.' That's the
> goal of this music - we need it to illustrate the Commando's emotional
> state. The Doom soundtrack glorifies the violence - our soundtrack must
> glorify the _intent_, and the intent makes the violence righteous. When
> mowing down weak enemies, he feels contempt, rage and pride, and concern
> for the time it's taking up when he needs to be getting on with the
> rescue. Against overwhelming odds he is stalwart, taps in to his deep
> righteous rage, 'I am a prayer for vengeance,' refuses to give up hope
> because he knows impossible odds are an illusion, this is a test sent by
> God and he _will_ overcome it. In calm moments he is praying,
> contemplating, meditating, proccessing the next mind map node - but he
> never forgets the urgency of the mission."

Derived mapping (at stated force) — the dynamic states are EMOTIONAL
states, not threat meters:
- **PRAYER (calm)**: contemplative chant/processing — over a pulse that
  never fully stops. Urgency inside stillness; never pure ambience.
- **CONTEMPT (weak enemies)**: the music does NOT escalate to full — a
  confident, almost dismissive groove (pride, impatience); the chant
  continues UNINTERRUPTED over the violence: trash isn't worth pausing
  the liturgy for. This is how "glorify the intent" sounds — the music
  stays on-mission, not on the bodies.
- **STALWART (overwhelming odds)**: the full congregation, the prayer for
  vengeance — and the harmonic floor HOLDS. No despair harmony, no
  collapse into chaos-diminished writing: it can strain, but it resolves
  (Dorian's raised 6th earns its keep). The cantus firmus does not stop
  during the peak — the test is being passed.
- The state classifier models how the Commando READS the fight (enemy
  weight class, HP, boxed-in-ness), not raw enemy count: many weak
  enemies is still CONTEMPT.

## The canonical track→state mapping (2026-07-13, his ear, verbatim)

> "Rejoyce... is the archetypal reflective chant, Fleshgrinder is the
> archetypal CONTEMPT ass-kicking music, Trash Planet Mining Rig is one
> step above it on the contempt - stalwart line, with a little prayer
> mixed in during the break. Not A Heart, But An Engine starts off
> prayerful, then a lead in that creschendoes in to STALWART+CONTEMPT at
> 30 seconds - then the slow, intense, unstoppable juggernaut-like saw
> lead at 1:00 goes to pure STALWART, back down to full PRAYER just
> before the 2 minute mark, immediately building back up in to CONTEMPT
> without losing the chants, the introduction of the guitar could
> represent a jump in intensity and enemy count. Then we get to the first
> central STALWART track - Styx Floods With Souls. Note how the saw lead
> moves _slowly_, but unstoppably, with breaks for chanting."

Derived, at stated force:
- **State exemplars for eval + composition**: PRAYER ⇢ Rejoice (01);
  CONTEMPT ⇢ Fleshgrinder (02); the contempt→stalwart LINE ⇢ Trash Planet
  (03, with the prayer-in-the-break move); STALWART ⇢ Styx (05), with Not
  A Heart (04) as the full state-arc demonstration (prayer → 0:30
  STALWART+CONTEMPT → 1:00 pure STALWART → ~1:55 full PRAYER → rebuild to
  CONTEMPT *without losing the chants*).
- **STALWART's melodic law**: the lead moves SLOWLY, unstoppably — the
  juggernaut. Long values, inexorable stepwise motion, chant breaks.
  (Augmentation — the liturgical device — is the natural rendering.)
- **Chant continuity across state shifts** is exemplified in the album
  itself (04's rebuild keeps the chants under the returning guitar).
- **Dynamic-system semantic he named**: "the introduction of the guitar
  could represent a jump in intensity and enemy count" — guitar entry is
  the CONTEMPT-onset voice over a persisting chant bed.
- States BLEND (STALWART+CONTEMPT at 04's 0:30; prayer inside 03's
  break): the classifier rides a line, not discrete buckets; stems must
  layer without exclusivity.

## Director-flagged gold from the talk

> "the 'infinite riser' - using Sheperd's tones to create a riser that builds
> escalating tension and release _on someone else's schedule_."

The dynamic-music primitive: a Shepard/Risset riser sustains "rising"
indefinitely — the GAME decides when it releases (combat entry, ambush
trigger, kill confirm), so tension always peaks exactly on the gameplay
event. Implement as a first-class stem (`shepard_riser`): intensity scalar
drives its level/rate; release = cadence hit + bus drop. Prime uses: a
dormant flicker nearby (the player can't see it — the music can), lockdown
waves building to breach, the drop into a hub fight.

## Working laws derived (shortest form)

1. **Start over entirely.** Round-1/round-2 instruments, melodies, and demos are
   condemned. Analysis artifacts (album block corpus, Gordon transcript,
   chant transcriptions) survive as reference.
2. **The named failure mode:** turning a creative brief into its most reductive
   technically-correct mechanical reading (organ clip + filter; MIDI guitars).
   The task is ORIGINAL COMPOSITION — the sources are inspiration to digest,
   not assets to fetch.
3. **Gordon's architecture is a spec, not a vibe.** Replicate component by
   component; where a component can't be replicated, substitute by *effect on
   the final product*, never by dropping it.
4. **The only quality gate is the director's ear.** No model score is
   authoritative. Short samples, early, over long polished failures.
5. **No subagents on this task.** The main line does the work directly, after
   directly studying ../johnny's music/audio workflow.
