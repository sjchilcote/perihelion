# The album's production language — raw-analysis extraction (no summarizer)

Read directly from all 72 per-block Gemini analyses
(`analysis/album_block_corpus.json`) on the main line, after the director
called the playbook genericized ("go back to the raw Gemini segment
analyses... it'll show how to accomplish the illustration of the feeling
with sound"). Quotes are verbatim from block analyses. Counts are across
the 72 blocks.

## 1. The mix architecture (the album's stage, ~30+ blocks)

> "rhythm guitars are hard-panned 100% left and right, while the kick,
> snare, and sub-bass are locked dead-center"

One stage, every heavy section: a WALL at the edges, a mono SPINE down the
middle (kick/snare/sub/lead). The center pocket is "carved out" /
"hollowed" for the things that must hit. Our stems are currently mono —
adopting this is the single biggest authenticity move available.

## 2. The space grammar — HOW FEELING IS ILLUSTRATED (~25 blocks)

> "massive, dark plate reverb (3.2–4.5 s decay, 20–40 ms pre-delay) applied
> to the choir and lead synth, while the rhythm guitars and bass are kept
> dry and centered"

The sacred elements (choir, chant, bell, sometimes snare) live in a VAST
DISTANT SPACE; the violence (guitars, bass, kick) is BONE DRY and
in-your-face. Distance = reverence; dryness = now. And the two are played
against each other dynamically:

> "The intro uses a wide, cavernous hall reverb (100% wet on the choir)...
> When the metal instrumentation hits at 15s, the mix instantly CONTRACTS
> into a tight, aggressive, in-your-face stereo field."

The polygon itself is an emotional actor: worship is far and huge, combat is
close and airless. This maps our states directly: PRAYER = wet/vast,
CONTEMPT/STALWART = dry wall + the choir KEEPS its distance above.

## 3. The pump (~25 blocks)

> "extreme sidechain compression, forcing the guitars, synth lead, and
> drone to duck aggressively whenever the kick drum hits — a pumping,
> claustrophobic breathing effect"

Everything breathes with the kick, 3–6 dB. The machine's heartbeat is
imposed on every other voice. (Also the Gordon-array logic at mix scale.)

## 4. The digital decay signature (~20 blocks)

> "a subtle bitcrusher or sample-rate reducer is applied to the DRUM BUS...
> gritty, degraded, 'Teleglitch' digital-decay texture" / "reminiscent of
> early 2000s PC game audio"

Bitcrush lives on the DRUMS specifically (12-bit, downsampled), not the
master. Plus: master-bus tape saturation "pushed to its absolute limit"
(~15 blocks), "glitchy bit-crushed edits (sample-rate reduction) at the
end of every 4-bar phrase," metallic SHORT spring/plate on the snare
("concrete bunker," "industrial warehouse"), ping-pong dotted-8th delay on
leads (~8 blocks).

## 5. The transition grammar (the CHANGE fields, everywhere)

- **Vacuum → wall**: ambience/choir alone, then the ENTIRE band enters on
  one beat ("violently shattered," "explosion," "crashes in instantly").
  No ramps. The album's drops are binary.
- **Wall → vacuum**: "the metal instrumentation completely drops out...
  a sudden, suffocating vacuum of space." The exit is as binary as the
  entry.
- Pre-drop tells: "a sudden, dramatic high-pass filter sweep cutting the
  low end entirely in the final 2 seconds," white-noise riser that EXITS
  at the drop, "ticking clock-like hi-hats" cut off.
- Mid-track rebuilds strip to ONE focused element (07 blk03: "a clean,
  pulsating, highly focused electronic arpeggiator and a deep bass drone
  to rebuild the tension").

## 6. Mode is moral register (across all tracks)

The chant/vow tracks (01 Rejoice, 07 I Swear to Protect, 10 On the Edge of
My Ability) live in **D minor/aeolian**; the violence tracks (Fleshgrinder,
Trash Planet Mining Rig, Populist) run **locrian/atonal** (C/D/E locrian,
"phrygian dominant," "tritone-heavy"). Faith keeps a stable tonal floor;
the enemy's material is the unstable mode. This is the album already doing
our emotional spec's "harmonic floor holds": STALWART stays D
minor/dorian; locrian belongs to the Adversary's texture, never to the
Commando's resolve.

## 7. The Chant-of series IS the emotional-state method

Three track titles are first-person vows: *Rejoice, For We Were Once
Blessed (Chant of Remembrance)* — *I Swear to Protect (Chant of Trust)* —
*On the Edge of My Ability (Chant of Focus)*. The album binds one inner
state per track and illustrates it (the director's Gordon-interview point)
— e.g. Chant of Trust: solemn choir alone → explodes into a slow crushing
groove with the choir HELD ABOVE the violence → a drum-less "ritualistic
male choir chanting a dissonant minor-second motif" over a wall of
sustained guitars (blk07/08) → the choir never leaves. Trust = the choir
persists through everything. That is the model for our cantus-firmus-
through-the-peak rule, straight from the source.

## 8. Immediate adoptions for our stems

1. Stereo stage: guitars double-tracked wide L/R, mono spine center.
2. Kick-keyed pump on wall/bed/choir in the combat states (3–4 dB).
3. Bitcrush the drum bus, subtly; tape-sat glue on the bus.
4. Dark 3.5 s plate on the holy-and-distant voices, guitars dry — but the
   assignment follows DRAMATIC ROLE, not instrument class (director):
   "sometimes the organ is distant and holy, and it has reverb. *Sometimes*
   the organ directly represents a machine screaming in defiance, and is
   close and raw." Two voicings per voice: SANCTUARY (wet, far) and
   DEFIANCE (dry, close, driven) — the defiant organ is a STALWART voice.
5. Binary transitions in the dynamic system: state changes are walls and
   vacuums at bar lines, not crossfades (entry-BAM was already the rule;
   the exit-vacuum is now too).
6. PRAYER stem: 100%-wet cavernous choir space (the album intro grammar).
7. Artist drop-in note: his own stems will slot into the same state map —
   the Chant-of tracks demonstrate he already composes by emotional state.
