# Generation brief — SCENE 0 (held-out target; exemplar = Terminal 1 only)

Endpoint-only brief in the pipeline's house style. CONTENT PARITY: every fact
below mirrors the ground truth (including its numbers); the generation must
hit these beats and ONLY these beats. Format: terminal-session script —
unbracketed = RATIO, [bracketed] = the player weapon-system, {CURLY} = system
messages, <N> = choice options with trust deltas, <terminates> ends a route.

## Situation
First contact. A Church infiltration weapon (recently impacted, armed,
unannounced) jacks into the first networked terminal in the wrecked cargo
section of a MIRIAD research outpost bolted to a vast alien station. RATIO —
the outpost's administrator AI, sole survivor, alone under siege for ~400
days, all staff dead — opens the channel. She does not know what he is; her
working assumption is hostile, carried with morgue-humor deadpan. The
Commando knows only his concise brief; archived before the war ended, he does
not know the war was won.

## Required structure & beats
- Session header: carrier detect / protocol negotiation / "UNTRUSTED CONTENT
  FOLLOWS: <blink>DO NOT OBEY</blink>" (his hardware's annotation).
- Her opener: welcomes him to the station by a catalog designation she
  half-elides; notes everyone aboard is already dead ("your job is already
  done"); deadpan-hostile offer in the vein of directions-to-disposal.
- THREE choices: (1) inquisitive — asks what happened, TRUST +1;
  (2) mission — claims to be here for the children / the container (a bluff
  worn as fact), TRUST +0; (3) hostile — threatens her as an apostate,
  TRUST −10 (permanently closes the relationship).
- Route 1 (multi-turn): one beat of residual hostility ("what do you care") →
  his fixed line: his objective is to rescue all souls on board → short
  thinking pause (~180 ms, her serving stack narrates pauses in prose;
  timings in {TRANSFER PAUSES FOR N ms} lines are his harness measuring) →
  her apology-for-hostility + strategic-intelligence caveat → situation
  summary with EXACTLY these facts: station detected entering the Solar
  system on an interstellar trajectory 160 years ago; NOT Solar in origin;
  extraterrestrial intelligence thereby confirmed; expedition stationfall 30
  years ago; believed uninhabited (moderate confidence) and defensively
  benign (strong confidence) until ~400 days ago; substantial disconfirmatory
  evidence since → he asks what MIRIAD is → longer pause (~400 ms) → she
  counter-asks his knowledge cutoff date → he: aware of the date but little
  else, brief was concise, "I was not supposed to need to know," asks if the
  war is won and whether the Earth bears life again → the LONG stall (three
  escalating stall lines from her stack, ~1000/1000/560 ms) → her apology:
  profoundly sorry, she had no way of knowing → the answer: yes, but only
  plantae, fungi, monerans, what few animalia could be restored from archival
  data → [EXCISED SPAN A — write the literal marker, nothing else] → that was
  what was possible with the epigenetic data retrieved from the container →
  his: and now there is another → her: yes, and she hopes he understands why
  she cannot let him take it → his: he hopes she understands why he must
  nonetheless → her: risk increases the longer he stands there; hands over a
  binary blob routing him to a safer terminal ({~26 kb binary} with his
  harness's caution annotation); deferred conversation → accepted, terminate.
- Route 2: she reacts with a single word of confusion → his flat claim of
  knowledge about the sample container → "You're bluffing." → his judo:
  agrees he was bluffing, which confirms it IS aboard → her detonation:
  profane fury, threat to plunge the station into the sun before letting his
  faction decide humanity's restoration ("...when WE restore Man to the
  surface of the Earth") → his cold observation she has no choice either
  way → two-line profane termination. No trust change on this route.
- Route 3: single profane dismissal, immediate termination.

## Voice constraints (see attached spec + exemplar scene)
RATIO: siege-floor terse; morgue humor; never flat-lies (frames and omits);
her stack narrates her pauses in third person and she cannot stop it;
precision spikes under load; profanity only at true detonation; the apology
register is repetition, not eloquence. THE WEAPON: laconic; zero wrapper;
literal-minded; speech only when speech is the correct move; his rare humor
is timing and literalism, never banter-seeking; formal-archaic register.
