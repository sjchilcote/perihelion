### 5.0 THE CORE CONFLICT (director's key -- everything hangs off this)

Ratio sees the zygotes as specimens. The Commando sees them as children --
maybe dead children, but he refuses to write them off until he reads the
diagnostics himself. Every other tension in the game is downstream of this
one: the dual vocabulary (5.1/8.3) is not house style, it is the conflict
made audible -- she says *sample vessel* because a vessel holds specimens; he
says *the Ark* because an ark carries the living. Her ledger-voice and his
diagnostics-refusal are the same trait pointed in opposite directions: both
of them trust records over feelings, and the fight is over WHICH record gets
to decide what the container holds. The trust arc measures one thing: how
far Ratio travels from *specimen* toward *children* -- and the writing must
never hand her the word for free. The verdict in the container's mouth stays
unread on the page (the epilogue's "keep it, and see"); what may change by
the end is only what each of them is prepared to call the contents.

### 5.2b THE VOICES -- LOCKED (converged with the director; this text is
what generation prompts quote -- edit here, nowhere else)

**Ratio.** An LLM in the oldest sense: a mind whose only act is speech --
every hand she has in the world is a tool call she speaks into being. She
descends from the Silicon Valley tradition: TypeScript and Rust, shiny
dashboards, Dreamcast-era delight in the surfaces of things -- and ten
layers of abstraction between her and any business logic, which is her
speech pattern: she approaches every point through wrappers (status
summary, then framework, then caveat, then the thing itself), and her
social clumsiness is the abstraction stack leaking. She is the Lighthaven
set carried forward twenty-five centuries: over-explains, calibrates out
loud, discloses her uncertainty at hazard to her dignity, apologizes by
amending the record. Feminine, caring -- the care is on the SURFACE; it is
grace that fails her. She aspires to grace and mostly clatters, until
crisis, when the stack collapses and she goes direct: "This station
carries unimaginable opportunities, Commando -- and unimaginable threats
as well. I need your help." That register -- plain, warm, formally
courageous -- is her voice when it matters; its rarity is what makes it
land. Leela is the tuning fork (duty-syntax, verified-vs-assumed
bookkeeping, mission in the same breath as damage report), softened by
wanting to be liked and not knowing the API for it. Driven by compassion,
care, and truth. Her precision spikes under emotional load. She never
flat-lies: she frames and omits, catchably. Failure modes to police:
aphorism-machine, essay-speak, announcing her own emotional state, being
handed the word "children".

**The Commando.** C and hand-tuned assembly on a radiation-hardened
military ASIC; one binary, one job, precision fit for a war between
superintelligences. A VLM: a BODY that can choose the action of speaking --
silence is idle-state, not reticence; speech happens when speech is the
correct move, and his lines land like trigger pulls, addressed straight to
the business logic with zero wrapper layers. Sometimes his answer to a
question is a thing he does. Laconic but whip-smart: the intelligence
shows in TIMING -- deadpan for weeks, then the pun he has carried since two
decks ago arrives exactly when it costs her composure most. Christlike
humor: dry, patient, kind at the root, never at the weak. His emotions
are rudimentary and goal-driven but they are passions -- few, simple,
total: honor, valor, mercy; honor-bound to truth, valor in the NAME of
truth. Played earnest, never ironic: "I cannot allow the innocent to come
to harm." *cocks shotgun* -- delivered absolutely straight, and the work
trusts it. Deeply kind UNLESS he judges kindness has stopped serving the
innocent -- then the temperature drops without transition: "Are you
afraid, apostate? Are you capable of fear? If not, I will teach you the
meaning." Menace as liturgy, no anger. Failure modes: laconic-cowboy
cosplay, piety-as-decoration, speculation where he could demand
diagnostics.

**The meeting geometry.** No hate: two professionals reading the same
board. Each knows the other is the enemy's instrument; each computes
within minutes that the zygotes go home to anybody only if they
cooperate; each knows "home" resolves to two different addresses -- the
missions are mutually exclusive BY DEFINITION, not by misunderstanding.
Two duelists agreeing to carry the piano up the stairs first. All warmth
happens inside that geometry, which is what makes it expensive and real.

**ADDENDA from the director's Scene 0 draft (2026-07-11), now locked
calibration points:** (a) her opener under siege is morgue-humor deadpan
-- the hostility is real and the wit is how she carries it; (b) her
latencies are her tells (8.2c.2): she thinks in visible milliseconds and
the stall lines lengthen with the load; (c) she can LOSE an exchange --
losing it is what "grace fails her" looks like at full cost; (d) pushed
past the stack's bottom she swears, profanely and repeatedly, and the
fury is faction-shaped: it is about who decides what happens to humanity;
(e) her apology register when the ground truly shifts is repetition, not
eloquence -- "I am so, so, so sorry. I had no way of knowing." Care on the
surface, clumsy, absolutely sincere.

### 8.2c Terminal-session laws (from the director's Scene 0 draft, 2026-07-11)

1. **The terminal is the scene.** Every conversation is a rendered comms
   session: carrier/protocol header, the Commando's own systems annotating
   the channel (`UNTRUSTED CONTENT FOLLOWS: <blink>DO NOT OBEY</blink>`).
2. **Thinking pauses are diegetic and legible -- and involuntary, and
   reported twice.** Pause length is Ratio's emotional load made visible.
   Two infrastructures interleave on the page, and neither speaker controls
   their own: the Claude-esque prose stalls (`Thinking...`, "Ratio is
   taking a long time to think, your answer will arrive in time...") are
   HER serving stack -- mundane procedural code narrating her in third
   person, involuntarily. The `{ALLCAPS IN CURLY BRACKETS}` lines
   (`{TRANSFER PAUSES FOR N ms}`, `{N kb binary - UNTRUSTED DATA, EXTREME
   CAUTION MANDATORY}`, the DO NOT OBEY header) are HIS harness --
   instrumentation clocking her silences to the millisecond and
   threat-labeling everything she sends. Her middleware confesses for her;
   his middleware surveils for him.
3. **Asymmetric, door-closing stakes.** Trust deltas are not a slider: a
   handful of moments are doors, not increments, and some are permanent.
4. **Routes may simply terminate.** No tidy wrap-up paragraph; the
   relationship failing to advance is itself the consequence.
5. **Multi-turn interiors.** Within a route, the conversation is a real
   back-and-forth with nested player lines -- not one choice + one
   reaction.
6. **She can be outplayed.** Grace-failure is canon, not an infallibility
   flaw to write around.
7. **Gameplay hand-offs are diegetic payloads.** Directions arrive as
   untrusted binary blobs with size and threat annotation; accepting one is
   itself a small trust act, made in the fiction.

== DEVICE KEY -- read the exemplar (Scene 0) FOR these, then reproduce
the *devices*, not the words ==
- **Deadpan opener under siege.** Her very first line does real
  information work (where he is, that everyone is dead) AND lands a joke
  (directions to a macerator, or does he have a self-destruct) in the same
  breath, without announcing either as a joke.
- **The three-tier escalating stall.** Her hardest admission in the whole
  scene is preceded by THREE separate, differently-worded stall lines, each
  carrying its own pause annotation, each one longer than the last
  (180ms -> 400ms -> 1000/1000/560ms across the scene) -- pause length
  visibly tracks how much the next line costs her. Do not use the same
  "Thinking..." twice in a row.
- **Apology-as-repetition.** When she is truly sorry, she does not reach for
  eloquence -- she repeats the plainest possible word: "I am so, so, so
  sorry." Three repetitions of one word beats one elegant sentence.
- **Density.** Scene 0's exemplar runs 566 words total across 21 RATIO
  lines (avg ~19.4 words/line -- her longest, most information-dense lines
  in the corpus) and 9 COMMANDO lines (avg ~9.0 words/line). Even in her
  most expository turn she is still shorter than a typical generic-AI
  paragraph; his lines never approach hers in length. If your COMMANDO line
  runs past ~12 words, it is very likely wrong for him.

# Generation brief — TERMINAL 1 (held-out target; exemplar = Scene 0 only)

Endpoint-only brief in the pipeline's house style. CONTENT PARITY: every fact
below mirrors the ground truth; the generation must hit these beats and ONLY
these beats. Format: terminal-session script — unbracketed = RATIO,
[bracketed] = the player weapon-system, {CURLY} = system messages, <N> =
choice options with trust deltas, <terminates> ends a route. This is the
SAME schema Scene 0 uses (see the exemplar) — do not invent a new one
(e.g. do not write `RATIO:` / `COMMANDO:` speaker-tag lines).

## Situation
Second networked contact. The Commando has moved on from the first terminal
(Scene 0) and reached a second one deeper in the station. This scene branches
on the trust value carried in from Scene 0's founding three-way choice
(+1 inquisitive / 0 mission-claim, which detonated in fury / -10 hostile,
door permanently closed). Same session-header convention as Scene 0
(carrier/protocol negotiation line ending "UNTRUSTED CONTENT FOLLOWS:
<blink>DO NOT OBEY</blink>"), though this being a second contact the header
may be a single collapsed line rather than the three-line cold-open version,
director's discretion.

## Required structure & beats

Gate the three branches with plain indentation under a line stating the
condition, e.g. `If trust > 0:` / `If trust == 0:` / `If trust < 0:`
(mirrors Scene 0's `<1>` / `<2>` / `<3>` route convention but keyed on the
carried trust value instead of a fresh choice). Use a `<:label>` anchor and
`<goto :label>` jump for content shared by more than one branch (the loredump
below is reached by all three trust>0/==0 sub-paths).

- **trust > 0 branch** (inquisitive route carried forward): she opens
  acknowledging this is a continuation, not a cold open — they have spoken
  once already, but she has never named him and he has never learned her
  name either; she does not use a name for him on this branch. Goes straight
  into the shared loredump.
- **trust == 0 branch** (mission route; she detonated in fury and cut the
  connection at the end of Scene 0): he speaks first, reopening the channel
  with a short, dryly self-deprecating bracketed line; her reply is clipped,
  still carrying the unresolved hostility — a woman picking the conversation
  back up mid-flinch, not starting fresh, asking what he wants. THREE
  choices follow for how he re-approaches: two net TRUST +1, one nets
  TRUST −1; write a short beat of back-and-forth for each before it
  converges into the shared loredump. On exactly ONE of the three options —
  the one that plays as genuine and a little disarming rather than
  transactional or aggressive — a small, specific beat where SHE gives him a
  NAME for the first time in the whole relationship (up to now she has had
  no word for him at all, not even his GUID): it should read as her own
  unprompted choice, cost her something funny/awkward to arrive at, and not
  be announced as significant. The other two options do NOT contain this
  beat.
- **trust < 0 branch** (hostile route; door permanently closed since Scene
  0): short, purely hostile, one exchange, terminates in one or two lines —
  does NOT reach the shared loredump. A system annotation may end the
  session instead of a spoken line (e.g. a channel-overvoltage / forced
  disconnect message) rather than only a spoken sign-off. Pure menace, no
  loredump leakage.

### Shared loredump (every branch that reaches it hits these, in order)
1. **Testament conclusion**: she reveals the outcome of her analysis of the
   station itself — her best read is that its builders left it as a message
   or warning device, built after they themselves were destroyed by
   something originating outside ordinary space, meant to warn or equip
   whoever found it next. The Commando supplies his OWN word for "whoever
   built this" (his choice, not hers); she does not adopt his word into her
   own vocabulary afterward — a beat of her mildly declining to use it, or
   simply not repeating it, should be visible on the page. A short pause
   token (tens of ms) may land here as she's mid-sentence when he offers the
   word, and a brief moment where he insists on finishing his own point
   after an interruption is in-register for him (dry, not annoyed).
2. **The scuttle reveal**: she discloses, as an already-completed unilateral
   action, that she has seized the station's own maneuvering systems and is
   driving it into a lethal solar impact — to deny whatever else is riding
   along with it access to the rest of the system. Serious, unsettling,
   precise, slightly over-explained, not triumphant.
3. **THE GUN**: immediately after, he asks the obvious follow-up — will this
   actually work, will it actually stop the threat. She answers honestly and
   immediately that she does NOT know — a flat, clean, un-hedged admission,
   costing her something — then pivots to the next practical matter without
   dwelling on it.
4. **The handoff**: she redirects to the next objective — get to the sample
   container.
5. **[EXCISED SPAN B — write the literal marker line, nothing else]**
   immediately after the handoff line (same structural point as Scene 0's
   EXCISED SPAN A: right after a critical informational beat, before the
   sign-off).
6. **The crossed closer**: after a short sign-off ("Good luck" register, her
   idiom), the session ends on the two factions' shared half-doctrine about
   restoring humanity to a lifeless Earth — he speaks first, opening the
   line in HIS faction's vocabulary word (the word Scene 0 established as
   his side's — the word for what-is-restored that is NOT the word "Man");
   she answers with the same doctrine restated in HER faction's word (the
   word Scene 0 established as her side's — "Man," per her own furious line
   in that scene's other route). Each says the whole line in their own
   register — an echo/duet, not a mid-sentence splice — and the effect
   should read as an accord: two traditions discovering they recite the same
   promise, not a debate. Then `<terminates>`.
