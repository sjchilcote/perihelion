**Session: `mappa`** — **Mappa Testamenti** (an illuminated map).

A medieval mappa mundi (the Hereford Map, the Ebstorf Map) of the
TESTAMENT, as the Church's chroniclers might have drawn it: the station as
a great circle (the ring) with the spine crossing it as a T (like the
T-O maps), its regions as flat illuminated vignettes — the Outpost (a
little grey tower with a crimson banner), the Gauntlet (a knot of
impossible stairs), the Museum (a building with a docent at its door),
the Chapel, the Hydroponics garden, the Core (a white sun-flower), the
Breach (a torn black mouth at the edge with violet beasts crawling out),
the Sun as a great gold face in the margin; tiny pilgrims walking the
roads between them; the ocean of space as the dark rim with the solar
sail as a ship. Flat colour, gold, crisp contours, decorative border,
a little abstract-mark lettering on scrolls. Square. Deadline 05:40.
