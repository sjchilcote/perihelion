**Session: `stylite`** — **The Stylite** (concept art for an ending).

One of the game's four endings: faith kept, his only companion dead by his own
hand, the mission failed and the vocation not. The Commando takes up his watch
**on the far side of the breach** — "a hermit-saint on a pillar at the end of
geometry, guarding a gate eternally, praying the hours by an internal clock,
for no one." Paint him there: a small figure on a pillar (like the stylite
saints of the desert), and around him the Adversary's space — a place without
consistent geometry, where things unfold, where nothing is ever static, in
cold, inverted, unnatural color. Behind him, perhaps, the gate he guards, a
last piece of the TESTAMENT's bronze. This one can be the most painterly and
the least literal of the set. Format your choice.
