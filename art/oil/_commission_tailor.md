**Session: `tailor`** — **The Tailor** (character scene; fourth docent at work; your last).

The Gardener, the Lamplighter and the Cartographer have a fourth sibling:
a docent who mends. Paint it in a small warm workroom lit by one hanging
lamp: the docent seated at a bench, bone face bent close, a bronze
needle in its jointed fingers, stitching up the Commando's torn crimson
tabard spread across its knees (the wire-mended bone cross on it); spools
of thread and bone pins on the bench; the Commando's frame standing
nearby without its tabard, waiting, helm slightly bowed, like a man at
his tailor's. One clear warm light, objects that read. 4:3.
