# Mappa Testamenti

The TESTAMENT drawn as the Church's chroniclers might have drawn it: a T-O map. The station is a great disc of parchment ringed in gold, set in a dark ocean of space with little white wave-curls, and crossed by the spine as a bronze T. At the head of the T, where Paradise sits on the old maps, is the Core as a white sun-flower with a gold heart. Above the arm of the T stand the Chapel (a small church with a red roof and a cross) and the Hydroponics garden (a walled green round with three fruit trees). Below it are the Outpost (a little grey tower flying a crimson banner), the Gauntlet (a knot of impossible stairs that climb into each other), and the Museum (a columned house with a docent at its door, its one amber lens lit). At the left edge the world is torn: the Breach is a black mouth in the rim with violet beasts crawling out of it toward the tower. In the margin the Sun is a great gold face with red cheeks. On the ocean of space the solar sail goes by as a golden ship under a red pennant. Tiny pilgrims walk the dotted roads between the places, one of them in crimson. Every place has a scroll of abstract lettering, the title and colophon have red initials, the corner carries a golden-angle rosette, and the scroll by the Breach has a violet smudge where a name should be. The border is crimson with a gold rule and gold dots.

How it was painted: as flat illumination. The border, ocean, land and T were laid at full coverage, then each vignette was built from clipped flat polygons in one or two colours, laid over dried paint so the contours stay crisp. Gold rings, roads, waves, lettering and pilgrims were stroked or touched on last. The gold rings first went on as brushed circles and came out broken and faint. They read only when repainted as thin filled bands. Still weak: the ship rides half on the gold rim rather than in the ocean, the Gauntlet's stairs read as a zigzag more than an impossible knot, and the lettering marks are uniform ticks.
