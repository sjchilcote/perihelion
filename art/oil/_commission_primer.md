**Session: `primer`** — **A primer plate** (in-game artifact / decal concept).

First read /tmp/cp/notes/fold_notation.md — the proposed meaning of the
Testators' fold notation. Paint one of the docents' **lesson plates** itself,
seen straight on, as an object: a bronze-and-bone tablet (or a wall panel)
on which the notation is carved and inlaid, worn by a million years of
cleaning, laid out the way the Testators would teach it — the escalating
series of seven lessons from the note (room-glyph and notches; a bridge; a
quiet loop with its upright inset; a twisted loop with its turned inset; a
mirror join; recursion as a golden spiral of insets; a fold of petals
around a heart), arranged not in rows like a human page but by the
golden angle around a center, with the final mark — the Breach, which has
no glyph — as an absence, a place where the carving stops or was cut out.
It must be internally correct to the grammar (an artist should be able to
lift glyphs from it for in-game decals), and beautiful: an illuminated
manuscript page, a reliquary panel, an astrolabe. Square or portrait.
