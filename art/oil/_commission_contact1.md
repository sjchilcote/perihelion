**Session: `contact1`** — **First Contact (I)** (abstract concept).

The Commando and Ratio make first contact. **One half of the composition is
concrete and physical: the Commando plugging a connector into a terminal.**
The other half is **Ratio's side, purely conceptual** — she is a mind whose
only act is speech; she has no body, only her abstraction stack, her
dashboards, her serving stack's "Thinking..." stalls, her ledgers and
framings, her loneliness. The question this painting answers is **what the
transition between the two halves looks like.** Your concept for that
transition: **it happens along the cable.** Follow the connector and its line
from his hand into the terminal and onward: somewhere along it, the physical
becomes the conceptual — copper and shielding and grime giving way to signal,
protocol, light, geometry, whatever Ratio's world is made of. Make the cable
the bridge the eye travels. (The channel is mutually suspicious: his systems
flag her words `{UNTRUSTED CONTENT FOLLOWS}`; she assumes he is hostile.)
Landscape suits a two-halves composition, but it's your call.
