# The Two Lampstands

The Testament is no longer falling. Over the upper left, the finished solar sail spreads like a sunlit sky, a vast pale-gold membrane with its rigging fanned down to a single mast foot on the hull. Under it, the station's bronze hull of scale-plates curves away like a small world. Two figures keep watch on that horizon, and the Ark lies between them. The Commando stands with his back to us: a cloaked pilgrim frame with a worn bone cross on his back and a staff planted beside him, the Sun drawing a warm rim along his shoulder. He holds a lantern low, and it is the warm lampstand. Ratio has no body, so she stands on the Ark as light. A small low-poly girl in cyan and white with long blue-black hair is projected from an emitter ring on the capsule's lid, and she is the cool lampstand. The lantern warms the scale-lips at his feet, and her light cools the ones around the Ark. Out in the dark to the right are two loose clusters of lights, the factions' fleets, one amber and one cyan, no longer firing and looking up.

How it was painted: The sky went down as chromatic darks of bone black, ultramarine and burnt sienna. The first pass was too navy, so it was repainted darker. The sail was laid as three graded piles of lead white, ochre and orange. My first sail picked up the wet sky and went grey and rocky, so I waited for the sky to dry and repainted it. The hull is rows of single strokes, each scale a dark sagging arc with a thin lit lip above it, widening toward the viewer. That simple mark carried the whole hull. The figures are opaque silhouettes, with rim light and the lamps laid over them after they dried. Two things went wrong with the paint itself. Ratio's face and body facets, painted over her still-wet hair, merged into one blue blob, and had to be repainted once it dried. A small scumbled halo around her went opaque and covered her completely. Six passes with a clean blender lifted it, and what was left is a faint ring that reads as a hologram's aura, so I kept it. With more time I would give the Commando's frame more machine articulation, so the pilgrim reads as a war-frame too, and make the far fleets a little more present.

## Second pass

At the director's request I went back into three things, keeping v1 as final_v1.png.

**The sail.** I painted out the soft gold blob and rebuilt the sail as a taut, square mirror-film stretched on four booms from a central hub, with its edges drawn slightly inward by the tension. The film is laid in long, straight strokes. Near the Sun's glare point it is lemon-white, then a band of warm gold, and over its far half the dark sky is reflected in it as slate blue. Long ruled streaks of glare run across the bright half. A hot hem picks out the sunward edges, and the far edge is cut dark. A mast now runs from the hub down to a real footing block on the hull, and the rigging runs from the corners to that foot.

**The hull.** I repainted the hull's body in depth: dark at the horizon, lit toward the viewer. Then I re-plated it with scales that each have their own width, sag, tilt and length of lit lip, so no two are the same. Near the lantern the lips catch warm, and near the Ark they catch cool, so a pool of light gathers around each lamp.

**The Commando.** An armoured pauldron, arm and gauntlet now come out of the cloak and hold the lantern. A small sensor mast with an amber tip rises from the helmet. Ratio's light puts a cool rim down his right side.

**What went wrong.** A first try at the plates, one filbert dab per plate, turned the hull into cobblestones. A thin scumble meant to soften the band seams went fully opaque and buried the plates, so I had to lay them again. The base pass also painted over Ratio's legs and the lantern, because both stood inside the hull's mask, and they had to be restated. The paint here is always more opaque than it looks on the palette.
