**Session: `shrieker`** — **The Shrieker** (enemy concept).

The shrieker is the game's non-Euclidean enemy: it "summons through
portals", runs a hunt "SENTINEL (patrol the folds)", its call audible
through portals before it is seen. It is a Testator of the lowest caste,
corrupted (handmade bronze and bone, golden-angle ornament, a person-shaped
absence), built as a herald or a bell-ringer: paint it mid-cry, in a
Testator corridor junction where three doorways meet at impossible angles,
its call visibly passing through the doorways (rings of sound drawn as
fold-notation arcs, or the air of each doorway shivering), and in each
doorway the first shapes of what it is summoning. Unsettling, sacred,
loud — a broken church bell that screams. Portrait. Deadline 01:45.
