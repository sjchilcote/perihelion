**Session: `firstframe`** — **The first frame** (concept art for the game's cold open).

The CUCULUS seed has struck the dead MIRIAD outpost and burrowed into its
cargo section: a scorched, armored projectile half-buried in torn deck and
crumpled bulkhead, its own hull opened like a husk, already eating the metal
around it (fabricator arms, glowing seams, raw stock being drawn in). The
outpost is dark and airless: no light but what the seed makes — the hot glow
of its fabricator, maybe the cold blink of a dead hazard light, a little
starlight through the breach it punched. From the seed's open fabricator, a
fresh **frame** (the Commando's body: a roughly human-shaped walking robot)
is standing up for the first time — steadying itself, newly made, still
glowing at the joints or still wet with whatever the printer leaves. This is
birth and invasion at once: the cuckoo's egg hatching in the enemy's nest.
MIRIAD language around it: right angles, cool grays, amber hazard striping,
stenciled machine-language placards, crates. Format your choice.
