# The Bone Garden

A long vault inside the TESTAMENT, ribbed like a greenhouse, its glass roof showing the deep blue of space at the corners and the Sun's corona glowing beyond the panes overhead. At the far end the vault opens on a doorway full of white light. Down the middle runs a pale path between two long-dry bronze channels, and either side of it grows the garden: not plants but living bone, grown slowly for a million years. Bone trees with branches that curl into spirals; sunflower-head discs on tall stalks, their florets packed in golden-angle spirals; fan corals spreading from a single root; nautilus shells coiled on the ground. All of it is pale ivory, lit on the side toward the far light. A great disc is cut by the left edge and a great shell by the right, framing the path. On the right, by the path, stands a small bronze bench. Halfway down, small and dark, the Commando walks away from us toward the light, his crimson rag the only colour in the place.

How it was painted: value structure first, on a warm mid ground. The vault went down in three tones with a pale bloom where the corona shows through the roof, then the ribs and glazing bars as dark arcs and lines receding to the far end, then the floor graded from light far to dark near, the path and the channels. The bone forms were set out in perspective, farthest first, each a pale ivory mass with a cool shadow side and a light side toward the far end, and their spiral crowns, florets, coils and contact shadows were added after drying. The figure, bench, stars, channel glints and rims went on last. What the paint did: the first band seams on the floor showed as hard horizontal steps until they were blended. The disc florets laid into wet paint came out as reddish polka dots, and only read as spirals once pale touches went between them on dry paint. The two big foreground forms dried as flat blank ovals until their shadows, florets and spiral grooves went on. Still weak: the glow around the far door is grainy stipple, the roof's corona bloom is blotchy, the fans are thin sunbursts, and the figure is a simple silhouette.
