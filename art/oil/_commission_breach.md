**Session: `breach`** — **Through the Breach** (Stratum Three concept).

The bottom of the game: past PERIHELION CORE, the Adversary's own space,
where "the invaders' space has no global embedding, no consistent
orientation, no 'outside'. Polygon-graph connections are maximally
exploited: polygons connecting to themselves, mirror-edges, spaces that are
their own neighbors. Air exists only where the player brings or makes it."
Paint the Commando's frame stepping through the Breach into it for the first
time: behind him the last bronze edge of the TESTAMENT (warm, solid, plated),
ahead a space in cold inverted colour where floors fold up into walls and
back into floors, a room visible from its own outside and inside at once,
fragments of corridor that loop into themselves, nothing holding still; a
small bubble of atmosphere carried around him, faintly visible, the only
air. He is small and very alone. Format your choice. Deadline 02:10.
