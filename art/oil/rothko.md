# Three Paths

The Commando's three paths as three Rothko fields, side by side on one wide canvas over a dark umber ground. **Vocation** is a deep crimson field floating over darker red, with a lower field of madder and a faint bone-white band between them. **Comprehension** is a cold ultramarine field over violet-black, with a thin pale band and a darker blue-violet field below. **The Void** is a near-black violet field. Its lower edge does not end: it folds into a smaller copy of itself, and that copy into a smaller one still. These are mauve, slightly too warm, and the only wrong thing in the picture. There are no figures and no objects, and every edge is soft and breathing.

How it was painted: 5 chunks, 2.4:1. I laid each panel's ground opaque with a soft edge, then the floating fields. My first feathering pass blended all the field edges in one call, and the badger dragged colour from panel to panel, leaving pink and blue streaky rims everywhere. I repainted every panel in deeper colours and feathered each field on its own: a light stipple of that panel's ground across the edge, then a clean narrow blend of just that field. Thin glazes of madder, ultramarine-violet and cobalt violet, blended wet, raised the inner glow of the red and blue. Over the near-black Void, the violet glaze came out *lighter* (as studio note 57 warns), so I repainted the Void opaque and feathered it again. Short of the brief's 8–20 chunks: the blue is more saturated than a Rothko blue, the bone band is a little streaky, and more glaze layers would give the colour more depth.
