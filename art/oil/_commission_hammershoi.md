**Session: `hammershoi`** — **Interior with an Open Door** (a Hammershøi study).

A third master for you: Vilhelm Hammershøi ("Interior, Strandgade 30",
"Dust Motes Dancing in the Sunbeams", "Open Doors"): grey, still, almost
monochrome rooms; doors opening onto further doors; one figure seen from
behind or no figure; soft, even, northern light; empty floors; silence.
Subject: a Testator residence aboard the TESTAMENT, a million years empty —
a sequence of rooms in muted bronze-grey and bone-white, doors open into
doors into doors receding, a good chair against a wall, a bare table,
pale light falling through a tall window onto the bare floor, and (if you
dare) one Testator docent seen from behind standing at the window, waiting.
Everything held in a narrow grey-warm value range; soft edges; quiet.
Thin, even paint; every chunk dried before the next. 10–20 chunks.
Portrait or landscape. Deadline 02:55.
