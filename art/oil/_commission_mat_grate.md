**Session: `mat_grate`** — **Floor material: MIRIAD cargo-bay floor.**

Read the rules below first; they override anything above about composition.

The outpost's storage bays: heavier-duty than the corridor deck plate (another
painter is doing that; keep the same cool MIRIAD gray family so the two sit
together). Open steel grating panels over darkness alternating with solid
load plates; painted load-zone markings (worn amber and white stencil lines,
a stencilled bay number or two — but remember every unique mark repeats with
the tile, so keep them small, sparse and well inside the canvas); tie-down
rings; forklift-tire scuffs and pallet drag marks running in one direction;
spilled fluid stains; grime. Utilitarian, standardized, kept alive past its
design life.
