# The Window Seat, Years Later

The third of the Window Seat set, after *The Crew's Window Seat* and *The
Window Seat, After*: the same nook, the same eye, the same objects in the
same places, years later on the trust path. The fairy lights are rehung
round the viewport and lit again, warm gold with a few red bulbs. The
Testament in the viewport now lies further from the Sun. The Sun is a
smaller white point with a softer halo in a deeper blue sky, and the bronze
curve is lit more gently. Children's crayon drawings are taped up all round
the window:
- the old station drawing, now one of five;
- the knight, a grey frame in a crimson tabard with a white cross, waving a
  little sword;
- Ratio as a smiling lantern with a yellow window and rays;
- a green beetle with six legs and feelers;
- a small sun with a long ship passing.
A fresh blue knit blanket lies folded on the seat and spills over its edge.
There are two mugs now, the red one and a child's small blue one with yellow
spots. A yellow children's book lies open face-down. Propped on the sill
under the viewport stands a small bone tablet with a fold glyph cut in it:
six rings round a seventh, the fold. No figures. Warm.

## How it was painted

I made it in 9 chunks at 4:3, by replaying my Window Seat chunks with
changes:
- the sky rings round a smaller, more distant Sun;
- the hull lit more softly;
- the blanket in blue knit;
- the paperback swapped for a yellow children's book;
- the headphones dropped.
Shortening the sky rings left a ring of sky unpainted, and cutting the
headphones also removed the drawing's paper. I painted the sky ring and the
paper back in. The new drawings, the second mug and the bone tablet went on
last, on dry paint.

Still rough: the bone tablet stands partly in front of the viewport frame's
lower rim, and its glyph is small. The drawings are simple line marks. The
blanket's fold is a flat blue block.
