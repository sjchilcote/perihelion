**Session: `babel`** — **The Tower of Babel** (a Bruegel study).

A fifteenth master: Pieter Bruegel the Elder, "The Tower of Babel"
(1563, Vienna): a colossal spiralling tower, half built and half ruin,
its tiers of arches swarming with tiny workers, cranes and scaffolds,
a harbour and a town at its foot, a huge pale sky. Subject: the
TESTAMENT under construction a million years ago — the Testators
building it as their last act: a colossal spiralling bronze structure,
its tiers of arches and scale-plates swarming with tiny figures and
cranes, scaffolds on the unfinished upper tiers, the solar sail lying
furled in a cradle like timber at its foot, ships (their little craft)
arriving with materials, and the Sun much larger in the sky than ours.
Bruegel's warm earthy palette, a pale sky, hundreds of tiny precise
details made of a few strokes each. 10–20 chunks. Landscape. Deadline 06:15.
