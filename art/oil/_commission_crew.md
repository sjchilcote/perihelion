**Session: `crew`** — **The Crew of Outpost Miriad-7** (a found object).

On a wall of the dead outpost the Commando finds the crew photograph. Paint
it — not the photo on the wall, the photo itself, as an oil painting of a
group portrait in the manner of a Dutch militia-company portrait or a
Rembrandt group (e.g. "The Syndics of the Drapers' Guild"): six or seven
MIRIAD crew in work coveralls (blue, with reflective bands, amber hazard
patches), gathered around the pallet in Dock 4 with the docking collar
behind them, some sitting on crates, one holding a mug, one the watering
can from hydroponics, one with the clipboard, one wearing the blue jacket
from your Dock painting; faces individual, tired, fond; warm work-light
falling across them from the left; a handwritten-looking caption strip
at the bottom (abstract marks, not real text). They are the people whose
lives your three "before" paintings were full of. Landscape. Deadline 03:20.
