**Session: `letter`** — **The Letter Home** (story beat, intimate).

The MIRIAD crew are dead; their quarters are as they left them. Paint a
crew bunk in the dead outpost, close and intimate: a narrow bunk with a
rumpled blanket, a fold-down desk with a handheld reader still showing a
half-written message (abstract marks, warm screen glow), a photo of
someone's family taped to the bulkhead above (abstract figures in a
beach-like scene — from home, sunlit), a mug, a pair of worn slippers, a
small potted plant dead and dry. The Commando's armoured gauntlet resting
very lightly on the desk's edge, at the frame's edge — he is reading it.
The screen's warm glow and a cold strip of emergency light the only
lights. Small, close, unbearably ordinary. Value structure first, lean
darks, lights last. Landscape. Deadline 03:20.
