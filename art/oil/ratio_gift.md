# The Gift

The third picture in the Ratio room series. The seed's fabricator bay is a tunnel of heavy scorched ribs, dark at the edges and warming to an orange glow at the centre. The round print bed glows at its middle. On the bed stands the body the Commando has printed for Ratio, wearing it for the first time. It is child-sized, white shell with bronze panels and bronze legs, and low-poly because she designed it from her own avatar. She has stiff printed black hair in faceted locks, big dark eyes with square highlights, and a small surprised mouth. She stands with her feet a little apart, not quite trusting them, and holds her hands up in front of her. The Commando's frame kneels before her with one knee down, his helm at her eye level and the crimson rag on his shoulder. His cannon-staff stands planted behind him. One gauntlet is held out, palm up, its fingers just curling, ready to steady her. The print light catches his edges and her chin, hands and hems. She is flat and pastel even in this dark oil-painted room, the same two styles meeting that the series has been about.

How it was painted: the bay went down as warm-to-dark rings, blended at the seams, with the ribs, deck and glowing bed laid over them. The Commando was built plate by plate as in PALMER: each plate a dark core with a steel mid-tone, then warm edges facing the bed and dark seams. Ratio was built in flat clipped fills as in the room pictures. The paint did what it has done all night: her face and arms, laid over wet black hair, came out grey, and had to dry for days and be repainted before they went clean. Still weak: the Commando's first plates read thin and angular, almost a stick figure, and were only partly bulked out. His legs are a little spindly. The deck is a flat band, and the outer ribs are plain rings.
