**Session: `holdout`** — **THE VIGIL (game mode key art).**

THE VIGIL is the game's endless holdout mode on a fold arena: "Things still
come through, some decades. They are met." A fold arena is a Testator
chamber of six petals around a heart, where every route adds a room that
was already there (see /tmp/cp/notes/fold_notation.md). Paint the Commando's
frame holding the heart of the fold — braced, alone, weapon raised — as
things come through the petal doorways all around him: Adversary shapes
(inconsistency itself, cold inverted colour, nothing holding still) and
corrupted Testator defenders, from every doorway at once, each doorway
looking into a room that is the same room. Spent casings, scorch marks,
wear from decades of the same stand. Heroic but weary; a watchman. Strong
silhouette, dramatic light (his muzzle flash? the cold light from the
doorways?). It's 22:04; deadline 22:45 — keep it big and simple. Format
your choice.
