# The Floor, Unfolded

The Testator bronze floor as the Adversary leaves it. The course grid and the inlay still run exactly on the 125-unit grid and across the wrap, but the inlay's bone has gone blue-gray. The plates inside the cells are inverted: pale, cold bodies in three tones (ash-blue, violet, sickly cyan-gray) with dark violet-black lips where the bronze should catch light. Roughly a third of them are turned in their cells and a quarter have slipped sideways. Fifteen are split by straight cold cyan cuts, each with a dark slip-seam beside it. The joints are violet-black, flecked evenly with a cyan static. The fold-notation survives in dark indigo, nested polygons turned by the golden angle, on about half the plates. Mean value is about 106/255, so it stays mid-dark under the game's lights; the 2 × 2 tiling shows the grid continuous.

How it was painted: the same handling as the bronze floor, reversed. A knifed indigo-violet ground; joints, then plate bodies in clipped body strokes; the lips laid as a dark band inside each plate's edge; the inlay ruled along the grid with a flat brush; ornament and cuts drawn stroke by stroke with a pointed round and a rigger clipped to each plate. The lips, painted into still-soft pale bodies, came out as hazy violet-gray rims rather than true darks. Since the game warps this floor at runtime, I let that softness stand.
