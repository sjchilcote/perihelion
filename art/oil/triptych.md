# The Testament Altarpiece (centre panel)

The centre of a triptych, to hang between *Saint Palmer* and *Holy Ratio*. It has the same dark red border and flat gold ground. The TESTAMENT stands frontal and symmetrical as the holy image, shaped like a great monstrance. Its bronze ring is the lunette: plated, its rims gold, a row of small warm windows round it, and eight fold-notation glyphs in gold around its face. Each glyph is a room-polygon holding a smaller copy of itself turned by its holonomy, with edge ticks, bridges between alternate glyphs, and one bridge that leaves a glyph and returns to it. A gold hub sits at its heart on four struts. The spine passes through it as the monstrance's stem, rising to a pointed finial with a gold star and standing on a stepped bronze foot. Behind it the solar sail opens as a mandorla in bands from deep blue to a pale gold heart, struck through with fine gold rays. Above, in a segment of blue heaven, the Sun is a gold disc with a calm sleeping face and a ring of rays. Along the foot, the docents kneel in two rows of small white figures with tiny gold halos, hands raised toward it. Below lies the sea of fire, the corona, as on both wings. Red abstract glyphs stand either side, as on the wings.

**How it was painted.** The icon method from the two wings. The flat shapes came first: border, gold, heaven, the mandorla's bands, the Sun, then the monstrance and the docents. After drying, all the drawing went on as lines, every one a short straight segment or a circle arc, so nothing ran away this time. Two small slips. The gold hub, laid over the still-wet bronze struts, came up speckled and was restated once dry. The mandorla's band rims, drawn as full ellipses, crossed the bronze ring and had to be covered there with short bronze strokes. A spine seam line also crosses the hub. Next time I would give the ring and the Sun the icon's stepped highlights and draw the docents with a little more character, kneeling in profile toward the centre.
