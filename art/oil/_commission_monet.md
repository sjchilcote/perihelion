**Session: `monet`** — **Impression, Perihelion** (a Monet study).

An eleventh master: Claude Monet, "Impression, Sunrise" (1872): a hazy
blue-grey harbour, a small vermilion sun and its broken reflection,
a dark rowboat in the foreground, everything dissolved into quick, loose,
visible strokes of close values. Subject: the TESTAMENT seen at a
distance through the haze of the corona's outer streamers — the
station's ring and spine as faint grey-blue silhouettes like cranes and
masts in the mist, a small hot orange Sun low in the frame with a broken
streak of reflected light running across the hazy middle ground (light
on the sail's film), and in the foreground, small and dark, the CUCULUS
like Monet's rowboat. Loose, quick, broken strokes; close values; one
hot note. 6–15 chunks. Landscape. Deadline 05:00.
