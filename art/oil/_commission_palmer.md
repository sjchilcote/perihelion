**Session: `palmer`** — **PALMER** (character portrait).

The set has no proper portrait of the Commando in his first body. Paint
one: full length, standing, as a state portrait of a knight (think Titian's
or Velázquez's full-length portraits, or a carved tomb effigy standing
upright): the first frame — "a roughly human-shaped walking robot", a
Church soldier's war-body, the apex of post-human combat technology, with a
crimson tabard and a bone cross; mission callsign PALMER (a palmer is a
pilgrim returned from the Holy Land, carrying a palm frond as proof). He is
faithful, terse, a weapon that knows it is a weapon; no face — a helm with a
visor slit and one cold optic. Give him the pilgrim's attributes made
military: a palm-frond device etched on the pauldron, a rosary wound on the
gauntlet, the lightning cannon held like a staff. A dark, warm background;
one strong light from the side (Caravaggio); the armour modelled carefully,
plate by plate (tapered strokes wet-into-wet, studio note 60). Portrait
format. Deadline 02:00. Spend the time on the figure.
