**Session: `gallery_far`** — **Two Wings, One Volume** (level concept).

TWO WINGS: "Two wings, one volume." Two Testator wings occupy the same space:
you walk the east wing and the west wing and they are the same volume, each
with its own furniture and light, interpenetrating without touching. Paint
a view in which both are visible at once: a Testator hall (bronze, bone,
good chairs, golden-angle ornament) in which a second hall of a different
character (a different maker's idiom: different arches, a different light
colour, different chairs) occupies the same space — its columns standing
where the first hall's floor is open, its arches crossing the first hall's
vault — each consistent with itself, impossible together; the Commando
standing in the middle, belonging to one of them. Make it read as two
coherent spaces, not a double exposure: the light of each hall falls only
on its own things. Format your choice. Deadline 02:05.
