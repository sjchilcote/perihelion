**Session: `seed_exterior`** — **The Seed, Burrowed** (place concept).

From outside and in cross-section, like a technical cutaway painted as a
picture: the TESTAMENT's hull in section — layer upon layer of bronze
armour, void spaces, ribs — and burrowed into it, the Commando's seed:
an armoured pod lodged among the layers, cut open in the drawing to show
its rooms (the fabricator mouth glowing orange, the rack of spare
frames, the archive with its four status lights, the small sacred
corner with a cross and a crimson cloth), the drill-tunnel behind it
sealed with scar-plates, cables it has grown out into the station's
ribs like roots. Warm interior lights against the dark armour; a cool
exterior of space above the outer hull. Clean, precise, readable, like
an old engraving coloured by hand. Landscape. Deadline 05:30.
