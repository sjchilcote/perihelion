**Session: `klimt`** — **The Handshake** (a Klimt study).

An eighth master: Gustav Klimt's golden period ("The Kiss", "Portrait of
Adele Bloch-Bauer I", the Stoclet Frieze "Tree of Life"): figures whose
bodies dissolve into flat fields of gold ornament — spirals, rectangles,
circles, eyes — with only faces and hands modelled; a flat gold ground.
Subject: the Commando and Ratio making first contact. The Commando's
armoured form in dark rectangles of steel and crimson (Klimt's
rectilinear male pattern), Ratio's avatar (pale face, long black hair) in
soft circles and spirals of pale blue and gold (Klimt's curvilinear
female pattern), the two patterns meeting and interlocking where his
gauntlet plugs a connector into a terminal and her hand reaches toward
it; the ground a field of golden-angle spirals and fold-notation
circles. Only her face, his gauntlet and her hand modelled; everything
else flat ornament. Portrait (square-ish). 10–25 chunks. Deadline 04:00.
