# Coolant Row, the Pump-Room Flip

We stand on a grated walkway in a dead MIRIAD pump room, lit only by our own helmet lamp. Two big identical coolant pumps tower on either side, dark columns with a pale flank toward the lamp. Each has an amber-and-black hazard ring, louvred vents, a gauge, and risers going up into the dark ceiling, with rime where the risers meet the caps. Ahead, the north bulkhead has an open hatch with a hazard collar. Its heavy leaf is swung back toward us on the left, showing its locking wheel. Our lamp lays a soft glow on the bulkhead around the hatch. Through the hatch is the next pump room, and it is the same room: the same pumps stand at the same places on either side. But it is flipped. Their hazard stripes slant the other way, and its far collar's chevrons run against ours. At its far hatch stands the Commando, his back to us, silhouetted against a room lit by his own lamp. His lamp breaks around his helmet and shoulders, and our lamp touches his back faintly. Beyond him, small, is another amber collar. Every room is locally coherent; the sequence is wrong. It is quiet, cold and gray, and only the amber is warm.

**How I painted it.** I wrote a one-point perspective in metres: two rooms with hatches at 6 m and 14 m, a hatch with a wall thickness, and a pump helper that paints each column in dark, mid and lit zones. Lead white in the gray mixes again came out much lighter than planned. The first near pumps looked like pale concrete pillars, so I repainted them in lean darks with a narrow lit flank. Thin amber laid with the detail hand over dark gray went olive, as the studio notes warn. A heavier body-hand coat fixed it. A frost stipple read as TV static and had to be painted out, so the rime is now a few broken drawn edges. A lamp-lit patch of deck came out as a hard-edged slab, and I turned it into the grated walkway it seemed to want to be. Next time I would build the rime and the lamp's falloff on the scrap canvas first. I would also make the flip harder to miss, for example with a stencilled number reading backwards on the far pumps.

## Pass at 01:01 CDT, Oct 2 (chunk 13)

The flip now lands. The near pumps carry amber stencils, P-07 on the left and P-08 on the right, painted down the lit flank of each. In the far room the same stencils stand on the opposite sides, P-07 on the right and P-08 on the left, and each letter is reversed: the P's bowl faces left and the 7's stroke leans the wrong way. The far hatch's leaf is swung back on the right, with its locking wheel facing the room's centre, mirroring our door on the left. Everything else is unchanged. What still falls short: the far wheel is very small and faint at that distance, and the reversed "0" and "8" are symmetric, so only the P and 7 carry the mirror.
