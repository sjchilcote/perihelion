**Session: `mirror`** — **The Mirror Ward** (visual-language concept for a game area).

MIRROR WARD, in the TESTAMENT's shallows: "The mirror room. Things come out
of it." One wall of a Testator ward is a **mirror join** — not glass, but
the room glued to its own reflection: the space continues through it,
reflected, and anything in the reflection is real and can walk out. (In the
game: "never shoot a mirror head-on" — your shot comes back.) In the
proposed fold notation (/tmp/cp/notes/fold_notation.md) a mirror join is
written as a reflected inset, outlined in bone, on the far side of a bar —
the Testators carved that diagram over the join as a warning to the
visitor. Paint the ward: Testator hospitality (beds or couches for
convalescent creatures? a ward is for the sick), bronze and bone, the
mirror-join wall with the room continuing beyond it, the Commando's frame
seeing himself in it — except that the reflection is not quite doing what
he is doing, or something stands in the reflection that is not in the room.
Local coherence, global wrongness: every part plausible, the whole
impossible. Define the area's visual language for the team. Format your
choice.
