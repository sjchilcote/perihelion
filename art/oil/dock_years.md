# Dock 4, Years Later

The third of the Dock set, after *Dock 4 — Sweep* and *Dock 4, Before the
Fall*, with the same geometry: the ring corridor rising and bending left
past the near frame. It is the trust ending, years on. The work lights burn
warm. The docking collar is sound, its green status lenses lit. The MIRIAD
pallet still carries its crate with the blue hexagon mark. Beside it stands
a Testator-made cart: a low bed of hand-beaten bronze with leaf-curled ends,
studs along its lip at phyllotactic spacing and spoked wheels, carrying a
bronze basin of seedlings. The bay doors have been re-stencilled by a
child's hand, in big wobbly brush numerals: a red 7 on the near door, a
blue 8, a green 9, and round the bend, painted carefully in amber, a 1 with
a little smiley face beside it. The count is still wrong, and now somebody
likes it. Chalk drawings cover the deck: a sun, a stick family of two big
and one small, the ring station with its pole, a spiral flower and a
hopscotch. No figures. Warm.

## How it was painted

I made it in 12 chunks at 16:9. I replayed my Dock 4, Before the Fall chunks
with every pile warmed (yellow ochre and burnt sienna into the greys, and
warm-white work lights). I dropped the jacket and the clipboard, and left
out the old stencils so the doors were blank. Then I painted the new things
on dry paint: the child's numerals (one stroke path per digit, with heavy
shake for the 7 and almost none for the careful 1), the smiley, the bronze
cart and the chalk. The chalk first landed in the part of the floor that is
below the frame and on top of the cart, so I moved it onto the visible deck
and repainted the cart and case tops over the stray lines.

Still rough: the room is evenly lit and pale, with little depth of value.
The chalk is faint at full view. The smiley is small.
