**Session: `spine_walk`** — **On the Spine** (exterior set piece).

Outside, on the TESTAMENT's spine, in hard vacuum light: the Commando
walking along the top of the spine toward the hub, magnetic boots, a
safety line clipped to a rail, seen from a little behind and above. The
spine runs away from us as a long bronze road of scale-plates with vault
bays down its flanks, converging to the hub; the ring rises on both sides
as a vast arch overhead (we're inside its circle); the Sun is behind us,
so everything is front-lit in hard white-gold with pitch-black shadows (no
air, no fill light, shadows absolutely black and sharp); the sky black,
a few stars only away from the Sun. Hard-edged light and shadow is the
whole picture. Value structure first, lean darks (pure black shadows),
lights last on dry paint. 16:9. Deadline 03:40.
