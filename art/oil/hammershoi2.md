# The Pews, Empty

This is the pair to The Long Watch Ends: the same chapel, seen from the same eye with the same coordinates, but with the watch kept alone. The light is cold and grey now. The end wall is a pale grey-white, and the window above the altar shows only a whitish sky with a faint disc of the Sun. Grey walls with dark ribs recede in perspective, each rib catching a thin cool edge. Every pew is empty, with dust lying pale on the seats and along the backs. The candle stands at the pew ends hold dead white stubs. On the altar, three candles are out and one still burns, the only warm point in the picture. A narrow strip of grey light runs down the aisle, and in it, small before the faded crimson altar cloth, the Commando's frame kneels with its back to us, the crimson rag on its shoulders.

I rebuilt it by re-running my own Long Watch Ends chunks with the same perspective scaffold, rib spacing, pew depths, altar and window, all re-mixed from warm bronze and gold into bone black, ultramarine ash and lead white. I left out the congregation and laid the pews empty, with a pale dust plane on each seat and back rail. Everything bright went on dry paint, and the single lit candle went in last. One mishap: my first chunk named a tube that isn't in this box, and the easel had to rebuild before I could continue. Still short: the near-wall rib edges are a little bright and busy for a Hammershøi stillness, the altar block nearly vanishes against the pale end wall, and the near pews are heavy boxes.
