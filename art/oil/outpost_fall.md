# The Night It Fell

The hydroponics deck, from the same eye and with the same geometry as
*Hydroponics, Before the Fall*, at the moment the power goes. The grow-lamps
are going out down the curve of the ring. The big fixture over our floor is
already dead, a dark housing with a violet lens of spent LEDs, and so is
the one hanging over the floor overhead. The next one down the ring is
dimming to a magenta smear. Only the smallest and farthest, at the turn of
the ring, still burns. Its pink-white light is the last warm thing left,
pooled on the walls where the ring bends out of sight and catching the
last leaves and tray lips there. Everything nearer has fallen into
blue-black. Red emergency strips have come on along both feet of the walls,
flickering in runs, and their light picks out the near leaves and the edges
of the trays in red. On the right, at the far turn, a door stands half
open. Beyond it is a violet light that belongs to nothing in the outpost,
and it lies across the beds in a long cold wedge. In the foreground the
crew's work cart stands with its mug knocked over and coffee running off
the edge. The yellow watering can has been dropped and is rolling, its
water spreading over the deck in a dark sheet that mirrors the red strips
and one fleck of violet. The window shows only the far Sun and the stars.
There are no figures; we see only what it does to the light.

## How it was painted

I made it in 12 chunks on a cool grey ground at 1.6:1. I reused the
earlier painting's geometry exactly: the hourglass floor, bed rows, window,
lamp positions, cart and can spot. Then I painted the same room dark. A
few things fought me:
- The pink light at the turn first went on as two hard-edged pink blocks.
  Graded rings round the turn gave a stepped target. One broad badger pass
  over the still-wet rings finally fused them into a glow.
- The violet door first came out pale lilac.
- Its halo went on as an opaque purple oval, and only a blend over it while
  wet turned it into light.

Still rough: the red strips are nearly continuous, so the flicker barely
reads. The near crop is a flat dark mass. The cart came out mottled, and
the spill reads mostly as a dark shape.
