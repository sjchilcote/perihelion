long low dark shape lying across the lower third, a single gold point of

a scatter of gold sparks (distant windows) on it, the sky one long blue
wash. Quiet, flat, decorative, Japanese-print composition. Thin wet
glazes, everything blended wet, then the few gold points dry. 6–15 chunks.
