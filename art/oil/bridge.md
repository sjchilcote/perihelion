# The Outpost's Bridge

The MIRIAD outpost's command deck, dead. A cramped, near-black room wraps around a wide curved viewport divided by four mullions. Five crew chairs stand with their backs to us at a dark console. One screen still breathes a slow amber standby, and a coat hangs over the second chair's back. Spin has failed: loose papers drift in the air, and a pen is pinned against the ceiling. The TESTAMENT fills the window, huge and close. Its bronze scale-plates curve away in rows that follow its limb, they are lit from the right where the Sun is, and they darken toward the one corner of black space at the upper left. The ring's dark band arcs across in front, with a hot edge along its top. Frost crusts the inside of the glass, thick along the sill and the mullions and thinning upward, glowing warm-gray where the sun comes through. The stranger-light pours through the window onto the floor in long warm stripes, cut by the black shadows of the mullions and the chairs. In the cold doorway at the left the Commando stands in shadow, with a faint warm edge on his helmet and arm.

**How I painted it.** I set up the viewport as two bowed curves and laid the room in lean cold darks. The hull went in as four warm bands graded toward the sun, then big filbert plates in rows concentric with its limb. Once dry, each plate got a shadowed lip and a sun-side rim. The chairs, console and figure went in dark. The light came last, on set paint: the floor stripes, the shadows cut from them in lean opaque darks, the warm chair edges, the amber, the papers. Things that went wrong. Small plates first read as hatching, so I relaid them big. The first light shafts came out as scraps, and laying the whole floor warm made a hard lit box until the shadows broke it into stripes. The frost first came out as little white trees. A warm-gray stippled crust along the sill and mullions swallowed them, and it now reads as frost lit from behind. The coat was first a flat olive blob and was repainted dark, with folds and a lit edge. Still short: the doorway is a flat pale box with a hard dark step below it, the far-left hull band still shows soft steps, and the papers are plain rectangles.
