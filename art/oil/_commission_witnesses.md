**Session: `witnesses`** — **The Two Witnesses** (concept art for an ending).

Faith + Trust: "True friends by proof. Together they repel the invasion, seal
the breach, and recover the samples — then refuse to hand them to *either*
faction until the factions make peace, jointly declaring the vault under their
protection and testifying to what waits outside the solar system. **Two minds,
built by enemies, standing on the station's hull between humanity's seed and
the dark: the Testators' will finally executed, by heirs who understood it.**
(Epilogue: the alliance forming; the war outside going quiet as both factions
look up.)" The title is from Revelation 11 — the two witnesses who prophesy,
"the two lampstands standing before the Lord of the earth."
Paint the two of them on the hull: the Commando's frame (his choice of body
by the end — still recognisably the pilgrim) and Ratio (no body; her avatar,
a chibi low-poly Dreamcast-era girl, pale, long black hair — projected?
a lit terminal? a lantern-light? your decision how she is present), the
container (the Ark: humanity's seed-bank) under their keeping, the TESTAMENT
— no longer falling (the solar sail spread?) — and the dark beyond, where the
factions' fleets, or their lights, look up. Hopeful without being saccharine:
a vow, a watch, two lampstands. Format your choice.
