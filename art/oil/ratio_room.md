# Where Ratio Lives

The room Ratio's avatar lives in when she talks to the Commando: a small, cosy, slightly wrong late-90s 3D room. Flat pastel walls (peach, cream, mint) and a lilac ceiling meet in one-point perspective over a checkered floor of cream and teal. A big window shows a sky in four flat bands of blue, three blocky pixel clouds, and a sun that is just a yellow octagon. A bookshelf against the left wall holds books that are flat coloured slabs. A potted plant with triangle leaves stands by the window. Ratio herself, chibi and low-poly, pale, with long black hair in faceted locks, a sailor collar and a little red tie, sits on a faceted lilac beanbag hugging her knees. She is turned toward a chunky beige CRT on a low table, mouth open mid-sentence, a speech bubble with three dots in the air between them. On the CRT's screen, under a blue session header with a red warning tag, is the Commando's dark helm looking in, a cold glint in its visor slit. In the far right corner the room simply stops: the wall breaks up into a horizontal dither of fog where the world isn't drawn. Faint scanlines run over everything.

How it was painted: as flat shapes on purpose. Each wall, facet, tile, slab and leaf is a clipped polygon filled with one pile at full coverage, and the light is carried by which pile each facet gets, not by modelling. Small parts went on over dried paint so their colours stayed clean. The paint made two messes. The first fog, a scumble softened with the blender, dried as an opaque white cloud over the corner, the window and the plant. A floor-light pass I forgot to keep off Ratio veiled her whole body in white. Both were repainted flat from dry: walls, sky bands, window frame, tiles, then her hair, clothes and limbs. The fog that works is the second one, short horizontal strokes thickening into the corner with no blending, which reads like a draw-distance glitch. Her face first came out grey, because pale paint over wet black hair goes grey, and had to be repainted after drying. Still weak: the beanbag's floor shadow is a separate lilac shape, the original plant is now hidden behind the CRT (a second was painted by the window), and the scanlines are very faint.
