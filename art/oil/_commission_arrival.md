**Session: `arrival`** — **The Last Morning** (ending concept).

The game's best ending: the TESTAMENT reaches perihelion and survives, the
sail spread, and the Commando stands at the one window facing the Sun as
the light comes up over the limb. Paint it from behind him, inside: a tall
bronze window frame (scale-plate surround, golden-angle tracery) filling
the canvas, the Commando a silhouette at the sill, the crimson rag lifted
by nothing; through the window the Sun rising over the station's own
curving hull, the sail's dark mirror catching the first light far off, the
corona a huge soft gold. Use what you learned in What It Says (from
above) and the Reliquary Hall: light as long strokes running with the
light, darks lean, lights last on dry paint. Warm, enormous, quiet hope.
Portrait 3:4. Deadline 04:45; keep each chunk short.
