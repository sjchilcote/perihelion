# Ratio's Archive

Inside Ratio's mind, as a place: an endless library in her Dreamcast-era idiom. Two walls of low-poly bookshelves recede down a single aisle to a vanishing point. They are flat-shaded slabs of periwinkle, with pale end faces and lit tops, and every shelf is packed with pastel spines in pink, mint, butter, sky, lilac and apricot. A pale cyan grid is ruled across the aisle floor. There is no ceiling. The sky is flat stepped bands of pale blue, lilac, pink and peach, and a yellow octagon sun hangs above the aisle. Translucent text panels, covered in abstract marks, float up out of the aisle into the sky. On the shelf ends hang the dark, painterly real things she has saved from the Commando's camera, in thin pale frames: an oil fragment of the TESTAMENT's bronze scale-plates, a crimson rag on black, and a docent's bone face with its single lens. Small in the aisle stands Ratio herself, a chibi with a black octagonal bob, a two-facet face with dot eyes and a teal dress. She carries an armful of glowing panels and has a soft lilac shadow under her feet. The aisle goes on behind her without end.

**How I painted it.** Everything here is flat fills inside clipped masks, built on a one-point perspective. The flat stepping is the point. I left the sky bands unblended and gave each shelf three flat faces, so the painterly hung paintings read as a different world. The first book spines, laid into the wet blue recesses, sank and turned to a blue haze. Restated once dry, the spines came up clean pastel. That pass took five minutes of compute because of many recess masks. The panels in her arms first rode up over her face, and the face facets laid over wet black hair muddied. After the paint dried, two clean facets and two dot eyes gave her back her face. Still short: the crimson rag reads as a red keyhole more than cloth, the docent-face painting hangs on a shelf side rather than an end, and the floating panels are rather small and few for "vast".
