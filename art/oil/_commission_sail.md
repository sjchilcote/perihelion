**Session: `sail`** — **The Sail** (concept art for a mid-game set piece).

The station was always headed for a close solar gravity assist; Ratio nudged
its pass into an impact, believing the Sun would kill the Adversary along
with the station. Mid-game they learn the impact would instead SUPERCHARGE
the invaders — so they must **build a solar sail** to avert the fall. Paint
the sail going up: on the TESTAMENT's sunward hull (worn bronze scale-plates,
rows of lit windows — the language of "Last Minutes of the Approach" and your
own "Impact"), a vast, thin, half-unfurled reflective membrane rising on
spars and rigging, catching the near Sun like a cathedral window or a ship's
canvas; tiny construction machines (outpost drones, subverted Testator
sentries put to work) crawl the rigging; the Commando's frame, very small, on
a spar. The Sun is enormous and close. Hope as engineering: a wager against
the fall. Make the scale enormous and legible. Format your choice (landscape
suits it).
