# Final Approach, from the Cockpit

The Commando's view from inside the CUCULUS seconds before impact, the pair
to *Last Minutes of the Approach*. A dark cockpit frames the picture: a
heavy sill, the top frame, and two riveted canopy struts splitting the glass
into three panes. A knotted strip of worn crimson cloth hangs from the left
strut. A small bone cross swings on its cord from the top frame. Below the
glass, four hooded instrument screens glow amber over a row of keys, a few
of them lit. Through the glass the Testament fills the view. The near curve
of the ring is a great bronze shoulder covered in scale plates. It is
darkest toward us and on the left, and warms toward the Sun side, where
the plates catch hot lit edges. The far side of the ring arcs across the
dark sky above. At the right edge the Sun flares through the glass in a
wide white bloom, raking scratches across the canopy and throwing one long
flare line across the panes. Projected on the glass in cold cyan, a HUD
reticle locks onto a darker breach plate in the hull. Corner brackets, a
ring and a cross-hair frame it. Angular glyphs stand beside it, with a
closing range of 0.48 and a chevron ladder down to the target.

## How it was painted

I made it in 15 chunks on a dark ground at 16:9. I laid the space and the
cockpit dark first. The ring's near face came next in graded bronze bands
that follow the curve, then about 1,500 scale lips drawn by a helper, each
oriented to the curve. The frame, panel and lights went on dry paint, and
the HUD, cloth and cross last. Several things went wrong:
- The sky by the Sun first came out in hard vertical stripes.
- The glare, built as graded rings, dried with a toothed edge, so I tapered
  it with an outer ring and a badger.
- A dark scumble meant to darken the hull toward us went on as a hard band,
  covering half the HUD and the target. A glow under the screens went on as
  solid bars.
- I repainted all of those as opaque graded bands, then redrew the scales
  and the HUD over them.

Still rough: the bloom is too big and soft for a hard sun in vacuum. The
hull's left half is flat. The long flare line reads a little like a cable.
The cross and cloth are small for the frame they're meant to give.

## Pass at 03:53 CDT, 2 Oct 2026

All of this went on dry paint (chunks 16–26).
- **Sky.** I repainted it near-black (bone black and ultramarine), with a
  thin deep-blue ring only right round the Sun. My first haze went on as an
  opaque blue disc and had to be painted back out.
- **Sun.** It is now a small hard zinc-white disc with a crisp warm rim, set
  just inside the right pane. My first disc landed outside the glass edge.
  Thin rigger streaks run out across the glass, and the long flare line is
  kept but thinner, with a few lit scratches.
- **Hull.** I regraded the whole near face in six opaque bands, from a
  bone-black bronze on the side away from the Sun to yellow ochre and
  cadmium on the sunward side. Then I redrew all 1,400 scale lips, with
  lit edges that strengthen toward the Sun. Both ring edges carry a hot gold
  line on the sunward side. A first attempt at darkening only around the
  HUD left a ragged blob, which this regrade replaced. The HUD, glyphs,
  range and target plate are redrawn over it.
- **Screens.** These are dark glass now, with amber graphics: a closing line
  graph, block numerals and dash readouts, a small schematic of the ring
  with a target tick, and a bar graph with a decaying waveform, each with a
  glowing amber edge.
- **Cockpit interior.** It is darker throughout.

What still falls short: the screens' edge glows overshot into rounded loops
outside the boxes. A few of the old streaks don't start from the new Sun.
The Sun sits in front of the ring's far edge instead of behind it. The hull's
middle band still shows a seam where the old repair was.
