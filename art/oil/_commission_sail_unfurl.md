**Session: `sail_unfurl`** — **The Sail Unfurls** (set piece).

The Testators' last hope, as engineering: the solar sail deploying from
the TESTAMENT's spine for the first time in a million years. Paint it
close and enormous: from on the hull, looking up, the folded sail
erupting out of a bay in vast pleated sheets of thin dark mirror-film,
each fold catching the Sun as a white-gold line, unfurling outward and
upward across the black sky in a great fan, spars shooting out along its
edges; small figures of docents along the bay's lip with raised arms
(directing it, or praying). The hull's scale-plates in the foreground,
hard vacuum light, black shadows. Use your light-as-long-strokes
technique for the fold-lines. Value structure first: black sky, dark
film, bright fold-edges. 16:9 or 3:4. Deadline 05:10; keep chunks short.
