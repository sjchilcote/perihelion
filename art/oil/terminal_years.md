# Terminal 0, Years Later

This is the same MIRIAD terminal, from the same viewpoint as Terminal 0 and Ratio Speaks, years later on the trust path, with the outpost lived in again. The corridor is warm: the walls are a lit ochre-bronze, a working light hangs above the terminal, and a run of ceiling panels glows down the side corridor. The vent beside the terminal is clean and dark, with no frost. Children's drawings are taped round the housing: a sun, the station as a ring and spine, a knight with a red tabard and a cross, a little girl with long black hair waving, and a strip of stick figures holding hands. Around the original red arrow and its smaller twin is a whole crowd of painted arrows in many colours and many hands, all pointing at the screen. On the CRT, in her window, Ratio's low-poly face smiles out, mid-word, one hand raised. From the lower right, a child's hand in a grey coverall sleeve reaches toward the keys where the gauntlet once did.

I rebuilt it by re-running my own Ratio Speaks chunks with the same coordinates. I swapped the dark blue-black base for warm ochre and bronze, left out the gauntlet and the frost, and widened Ratio's mouth into a smile. Then I laid the lights, drawings, arrows and the child's hand on dry paint. One problem: the old pink-dark spill from the screen came out as a dirty halo on the warm wall. I repainted the wall around the housing, which also covered the light fixture, the red arrows and some of the new arrows, so I restated those and painted a second crowd of arrows. Still short: the crayon marks on the drawings are faint at full view, a few arrows run over the drawings, the child's hand is a little stiff, and Ratio's window is the same as in Ratio Speaks except for the smile.
