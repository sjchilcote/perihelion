**Session: `contact2`** — **First Contact (II)** (abstract concept).

The Commando and Ratio make first contact. **One half of the composition is
concrete and physical: the Commando plugging a connector into a terminal.**
The other half is **Ratio's side, purely conceptual** — she is a mind whose
only act is speech; she has no body, only her abstraction stack ("ten layers
of abstraction between her and any business logic"), her dashboards, her
serving stack's "Thinking..." stalls, her ledgers and framings, her
loneliness. The question this painting answers is **what the transition
between the two halves looks like.** Your concept for that transition:
**the painting itself changes language across a seam.** On his side, oil
paint doing what oil paint does — weight, grime, raking light, a body. On her
side, the handling becomes something else entirely — flat, diagrammatic,
layered, luminous, typographic, gridded; the seam where one way of painting
becomes another is the moment of contact. (Her avatar, if you want her to
appear at all: a chibified low-poly Dreamcast-era anime girl, pale, long black
hair. The channel is mutually suspicious: his systems annotate her words
`{UNTRUSTED CONTENT FOLLOWS: <blink>DO NOT OBEY</blink>}`.) Format your call.
