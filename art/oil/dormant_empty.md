# The Dormitory, Empty

This is the third of the Dormitory set: the same gallery from the same eye, with the same coordinates as The Dormitory and One Wakes, later. Every alcove is empty. In each one, claw scratches run in clusters of three slashes, torn cradle brackets stick out at the sides, and a severed cable hangs from the top. No eyes are lit anywhere. The deck is scored all over with many-legged tracks. They run in wandering lanes down the whole length of the gallery toward the far end. There the amber hazard lamp hangs dead, an empty housing with a broken shard and a loose wire, and the doorway stands open on violet light, its leaf swung back and a cold violet wedge lying on the floor in front of it. The Commando stands small at the near end of the aisle with his back to us, his shoulder lamp full on. Its warm pool runs out ahead of him along the aisle and picks the tracks out of the dark, and his edges catch the violet from the door.

I rebuilt the gallery by re-running my Dormitory chunks for the walls, floor and alcove placement. I left the Wardens out, and the scratches, fittings and cables went into each alcove in its own wall-plane coordinates. The tracks were laid as rows of short jointed marks: dark scores everywhere, with lit lips inside the lamp's pool. Two mistakes cost time. I re-used my old figure chunk, which still contained a pool painted in the same chunk as the figure, so he came out translucent again. I first laid the beam's pool behind him instead of ahead, and had to paint the floor back, repaint him on dry paint and lay the pool forward. Still short: the floor near the bottom edge still carries a faint residue of the misplaced pool, the scratches are a little uniform, and the figure is a plain dark silhouette.
