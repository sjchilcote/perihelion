**Session: `rack`** — **The Rack** (character concept: how the Commando's frame changes).

Frames are expendable; the mind is not. Spare bodies are fabricated in
advance and hung in the seed's rack; every death, the mind steps into the
next. And the frames change radically over the game: from the apex of
post-human combat technology — a roughly human-shaped Church soldier — to
something beyond comprehension: a centauroid frame bristling with weapons,
manipulators and sensor masts, and on the apostasy path, Adversary organs
grafted in. Paint the rack inside the CUCULUS seed (a cramped, hot,
fabricator-lit space; it is home, memory and monastery): a row of hanging
frames receding in sequence, each generation stranger than the last, from a
human-shaped frame nearest us to something at the far end of the rack that
we can barely make out and don't want to. It should work as a concept sheet
(the frames read as distinct designs) and as a painting (dread growing down
the row). Perhaps a votive detail: a crimson cloth, a cross, a rosary hung
on the first hook. Format your choice (landscape suits a row).
