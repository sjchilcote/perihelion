# The Gauntlet

A square bronze corridor recedes in one-point perspective to a warm far end. At two places partway down, thin violet seams trace the corridor's whole cross-section, each with a slit of violet light hanging vertically in the air. Beyond the first seam the same corridor carries on rotated a quarter turn: its grey deck grating now runs up the right wall, its ceiling is the dark left wall, and its lamps hang sideways, throwing their warm pools onto the right-hand "floor". Beyond the second seam it turns again, so the grating is overhead and the lamps hang upward. Every plate seam and grating line carries straight across both seams without a break, so the geometry is consistent, just glued wrong. In the near end the Commando stands small on the deck grating with his back to us, in his red tabard with the grey cross, looking down it.

How it was painted: the tunnel method. I laid the corridor first as three segments of four perspective faces each. Each face got the material of its role in that segment (grating for floor, bronze plate for walls, dark bronze for ceiling), so the rotation is carried by the materials alone. On dry paint came the longitudinal plate seams (continuous through all three segments), the transverse joints and grating bars, the hanging lamps with their pools, the violet seam outlines with a spill of violet ticks either side, and the figure. What falls short: the rotation reads mostly from the grey grating changing walls. The sideways and upside-down lamps are small and easy to miss, and the far segment is too small for its rotation to read on its own. The near segment is mostly cropped, so the first seam frames the picture more than it sits in it. The violet halo ticks look a little like stitching.

## Pass at 04:48 CDT

What changed (chunks 6–11), all on dry paint. **Seams:** the violet ticks are painted out, and the plate and grating lines are carried back across each joint. Each seam is now a single crisp violet line round the section, with a faint glow and the vertical slit in the air. **Lamps:** seven larger lamps hang from each segment's own ceiling, each a bronze cage with amber glass and a hot core. They hang down from the top in the near segment, sideways from the left wall (chains horizontal) in the second, and upward from the floor in the third. Each throws a warm pool onto its segment's floor (bottom, right wall, top). **Objects:** a bronze crate stands on each segment's floor: upright near us, on the right wall in the second segment, on the ceiling in the third. **Commando:** repainted after the seam pass washed over him, with his shadow thrown back across the grating.

What still falls short: the third segment's lamps and crate are small, and its rotation reads mostly from the crate. The repainted seam bands show as slightly lighter frames, and stray thin outline curves trail off the crates. The cross on the tabard is a little soft.
