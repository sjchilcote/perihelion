**Session: `hull_garden`** — **The Bone Garden** (place concept).

The Testators grew a garden — not of plants: of bone. Inside the
TESTAMENT a long greenhouse-like vault under a ribbed glass roof (dim
starlight and the Sun's corona glow beyond), where slow sculptural forms
of living bone have grown for a million years in golden-angle spirals:
bone trees with spiral branches, fan-coral shapes, sunflower-head discs,
shells, all pale ivory catching the light; bronze watering channels
between them, long dry; a small bronze bench; the Commando walking the
path between them, small, his crimson rag the only colour. Pale, quiet,
strange, beautiful. Value structure first (mid-dark vault, pale bone
forms), lights last on dry paint. Any format. Deadline 04:15.
