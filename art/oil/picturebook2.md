# Ratio and the Big Knight, page 2

The next spread of the picture book, in the same flat gouache shapes, warm palette, gutter and text lines as page 1.

On the left page, under a dusky lilac sky, a big purple bug with a row of blue spikes looms in from the right. It is a Warden, simplified until it is almost cute, with two big round worried eyes and two little fangs. The big knight stands square in front of it with his arms out wide: grey helm, dark visor, one warm dot of light, crimson tabard with a pale cross. Behind his outstretched arm tiny Ratio peeks out, black-haired and white-dressed.

On the right page, under a peach sky and the big warm sun, the same bug is curled up asleep in a round purple heap, eyes shut in little arcs and a trail of violet Zzz rising off it. Ratio stands at its head, smiling, one hand out patting it. At the right the knight sits with his legs out in front of him, having a rest.

How it was painted: page grounds, then the big flat shapes (bug, knight, sun), then visors, crosses and Ratio's hair as darks, and after days of drying, the pale faces, dresses and eyes, then the small marks and text lines. As on page 1, pale paint over dark paint that was still wet came out grey. Ratio's faces and dresses had to be repainted at heavy coverage, and so did the bug's pupils, laid into its wet white eyes. Still weak: the knight's grey is cool and a little greenish, the left bug crowds the gutter, and a faint grey smear remains on Ratio's patting arm.
