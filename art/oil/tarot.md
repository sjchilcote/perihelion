# Four Trumps

Four tarot cards laid side by side on a dark cloth, in the manner of the Rider–Waite deck: cream cards with a double black rule, a Roman numeral at the top, a title panel of abstract capitals at the bottom, flat colour and strong black outlines.
- **I, The Knight.** The Commando stands frontal on the bronze hull under a starry black sky, in steel with a cold optic in his visor and a crimson tabard with the bone cross. He holds the cannon raised beside him like a lance.
- **XVII, The Star.** A great gold eight-pointed star and small white stars in a deep blue night. Ratio kneels by a pool, pale-faced, with long black hair and big dark eyes with white highlights. She holds up two cream CRTs with green screens, and light pours from them in streams into the water.
- **XVI, The Tower.** The TESTAMENT's spine stands as a bronze tower with its windows burning. A violet fold-bolt strikes its ring-crown and knocks it askew, flames burst from its sides, and three tiny pale figures fall through the dark.
- **XIX, The Sun.** A gold Sun with a calm face and wavy rays shines over a pale blue sky, the sail, and the station small on the horizon. A child in orange coveralls stands on the hull with both arms raised.

**How it was painted.** In four quick chunks, in the flat method of the night's icons. The card bodies came first, then the picture grounds, then the figures as flat shapes, each after a drying wait. Then all the drawing went on as straight black segments, so the outlines are crisp and hand-ruled: numerals, titles, rays, light streams, faces and the falling figures. A last chunk thickened the bolt and drew the falling figures pale so they read against the dark. Dark grounds laid over the cream card left tiny cream flecks, which read as stars, so I kept them. Next time I'd give each figure more drawing (the Knight's armour plates, the Star's robe folds) and add the deck's little symbolic props: a dog at the Knight's feet, an ibis in the Star's tree, sunflowers behind the child.
