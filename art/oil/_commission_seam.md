**Session: `seam`** — **The Seam** (concept art for a level set piece).

Inside. The MIRIAD research outpost's clean, cramped, prefabricated corridor —
right angles, cool structural grays, amber hazard striping, a hand-painted
warning placard, scuffs and patched repairs, everything dark and dead — ends at
an aperture **cut into a wall of alien material**: worn bronze, scale-plates
laid in golden-angle spirals, edges rounded by a million years. The cut is
ringed with the researchers' human instruments — sensors, sample scrapers,
cables, placards. Through the aperture: the TESTAMENT's interior, warm-lit,
vaulted, built for creatures (good chairs; uncanny thoughtfulness) — and,
if you can, a hint that it holds more than its walls should. A small figure
(the Commando's frame) may stand at the threshold. Two civilizations meeting
at one edge. Format your choice.
