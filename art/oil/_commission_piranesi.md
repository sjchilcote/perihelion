**Session: `piranesi`** — **Carceri: the Gauntlet** (a Piranesi study).

A sixth master: Giovanni Battista Piranesi's "Carceri d'invenzione"
(Imaginary Prisons, 1750/1761) — vast vaulted interiors of stairs, bridges,
arches and galleries that cannot all be consistent, tiny figures on
impossible stairways, ropes and pulleys, deep shadow and hard light,
rendered in etched line. Subject: the Gauntlet — the game's non-Euclidean
proving-ground level — as a Carceri plate: bronze stairways that climb to
landings that are also the floors they started from, bridges crossing
under themselves, arches seen through arches, a portal seam glimpsed in a
far gallery; three or four tiny Commando figures (all the same one, at
different points in the same loop). Monochrome: a warm sepia ink on a
pale toned ground, built from hatching and line with a few broad dark
washes for the deepest shadows. Portrait. 10–25 chunks. Deadline 03:30.
