**Session: `museum_hall`** — **The Hall of Civilizations** (place concept).

The Testators kept, in their museum, an exhibit for every civilization
they had warned (or failed to warn) — each a vitrine holding one object
of that people, labelled in fold notation, most of them now the only
remaining trace of an entire species. Paint the hall: a long, high
gallery with tall glass-and-bronze vitrines in two rows, each lit from
within by a small cold lamp, each holding something strange (a carved
spiral shell-instrument, a crystal lattice, a woven thing, a mask, a
seed, an empty case with only a label), receding to a far case that is
empty and freshly prepared, its label blank — waiting for humanity. A
docent stands beside that last case with a stylus raised. The Commando
small in the foreground, looking. Cold vitrine glows on warm dark bronze.
Value structure first, lean darks, lights last. Any format. Deadline 03:30.
