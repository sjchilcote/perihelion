**Session: `bridge`** — **The Outpost's Bridge** (place concept).

The MIRIAD outpost's command deck, dead: a small cramped bridge, five
crew chairs facing a wide curved viewport, consoles dark except one that
still cycles a slow amber standby pulse, frost on the inside of the
glass in feathered patterns, loose papers and a floating pen frozen
against the ceiling (zero-g since the spin failed), a coat still on a
chair-back. Through the viewport, filling it: the TESTAMENT, huge and
close — bronze scale-plates, the ring's curve, lit by the Sun from the
right — its warm light pouring through the frost onto the dark bridge in
long shafts. The Commando standing in the doorway at left, in shadow.
Warm stranger-light on a cold dead room. Value structure first, lean
darks, lights last on dry paint. 16:9. Deadline 04:30.
