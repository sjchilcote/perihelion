**Session: `disclosure`** — **The Disclosure** (abstract concept for the trust arc's climax).

On the trust path, the climax of the relationship between the Commando and
Ratio is **mutual disclosure of true utility functions**: "the machine
equivalent of total vulnerability, allowing each to formally verify the
other's values and construct a provably firm alliance. For beings like these
it is more intimate than anything bodies could do. It is not done lightly and
it cannot be faked." Two monks of rival orders in one monastery; never
romance-coded; "something for which friendship is the only available word."
He is C and hand-tuned assembly on a radiation-hardened military ASIC — one
binary, one job — a soldier of the Church whose rules ARE him (just-war
doctrine, the hours, the oath, the children). She is a mind whose only act is
speech, ten layers of abstraction deep, whose utility function holds restored
humans full of knowledge as the point of the universe. Each has spent the game
annotating the other as UNTRUSTED. Paint the moment each lays open the thing
at their core to the other — and the other checks it, and it holds. How you
make "formal verification" and "vulnerability" visible is your decision:
two structures opened like reliquaries? his `{UNTRUSTED}` braces coming
down? her avatar and his frame, or no figures at all? Her visual world:
Dreamcast-era surfaces, dashboards, low-poly, the grid; his: oil-dark metal,
the cross, the rosary, the oath in hard assembly. Format your choice.
