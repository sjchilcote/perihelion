# Ratio and the Big Knight: cover

The cover of the picture book, in the same flat gouache shapes and warm palette as the three spreads. A crimson title band across the top carries RATIO AND THE BIG KNIGHT in hand-drawn cream capitals on two lines. Below it, under a peach sky with the big warm sun, the gold ring curves behind a soft green-ochre ground. The big kind knight sits facing us: his round helm with its dark visor band, his one warm dot of light turned toward Ratio, his crimson tabard with a pale cross, and his big plated arms and legs. Tiny Ratio stands on his knee against the red of the tabard, in her white dress, smiling, with her black hair down and one arm up waving, two little motion marks beside her hand. At his feet the sleepy purple bug is curled with its blue spikes up and its eyes shut. A short line of abstract marks sits as the author line on the cream foot.

How it was painted: this time every shape was laid as its own piece of the page, side by side on the bare cream ground, with nothing painted over another wet colour. The band, sun, ring, ground and sky went first around the reserved figures. Then came the knight, tabard, visor, bug and Ratio's hair. After five days' drying came the pale shapes: Ratio's face and dress, the cross and the eye-light. A slightly deeper warm grey went over the knight once he was dry, so he stands off the cream. Then the eyes, seams and lettering. Ratio and the cross came out clean on the first try. Still weak: a faint ghost of the knight's shin shows through the left end of the bug, the knight's grey is a little dull, and the T and I of RATIO are spaced a touch wide.
