**Session: `rothko`** — **Three Paths** (a Rothko triptych, as one canvas).

A fifth master: Mark Rothko's colour-field paintings (e.g. "No. 61 (Rust
and Blue)", the Seagram murals): soft-edged rectangles of colour floating
on a coloured ground, edges feathered and breathing, thin layered glazes
so the colour glows from within. Subject: the Commando's three paths in
the mind map, each as one Rothko field side by side on a wide canvas (or
three stacked rectangles on one tall canvas — your choice): VOCATION (deep
crimson over a darker red, a faint bone-white band), COMPREHENSION (cold
blue over violet-black, a thin pale band), VOID (a near-black violet
field whose lower edge is subtly folding into a smaller copy of itself —
the only "wrong" thing). No figures, no objects. Many thin glazes, every
edge feathered by blending, let each layer dry. 8–20 chunks. Deadline 03:20.
