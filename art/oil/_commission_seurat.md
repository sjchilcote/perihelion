**Session: `seurat`** — **A Sunday on the Ring** (a Seurat study; your last).

A seventeenth master: Georges Seurat, "A Sunday Afternoon on the Island
of La Grande Jatte" (1884–86): a calm promenade by water, many still
figures in profile, parasols, a little dog, strong cast shadows, every
form built of small dots of pure colour. Your speckled stroke is
pointillism already — use it on purpose. Subject: a Sunday on the
TESTAMENT's ring, a million years ago, while the Testators lived:
a long terrace along the inside of the ring under the gentle light of a
far Sun, Testators (long skulls, bone and bronze) standing and sitting
in stiff calm profile, one with a parasol, a small jointed creature on a
leash, docents in the background, the ring curving up into the sky
behind them. Every form made of small dots of pure colour. Landscape 3:2.
