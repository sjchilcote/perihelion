# Saint Palmer

The Commando's first frame as a warrior-saint, in the manner of a Russian icon. He stands frontal and hieratic on a flat gold ground inside a dark red border. His face is the great helm: a dark steel crown with a cross-shaped visor and one cold white optic at its centre, set in a large gold halo with incised rays and a double rim. Broad steel pauldrons with gold assist lines are on his shoulders, and his steel arms are segmented into lames. A crimson tabard falls to his feet in long folds touched with gold, with the bone cross on his breast. In his right hand he holds the lightning cannon upright like a lance, coiled in cold light, with a spark of lightning at its tip. In his left he holds a palm frond, the pilgrim's proof. Below his feet, small, the TESTAMENT's ring lies on a sea of fire, the corona, its opening filled with gold. Above, in a segment of blue heaven, three small white-robed angels, the docents. Lines of abstract red lettering, each under a titlo bar, sit either side of the halo.

**How it was painted.** As an icon is made, in flat shapes first and lines last on dry paint. The border, the gold ground, the heaven and the halo went in as flat body colour, then every figure shape in one flat colour each, and after the paint dried, all the drawing went on as rigger and flat-brush lines: the outlines, incised rays, assist hatching on the drapery and armour, folds, the cross, coils, palm leaflets, flame tongues and lettering. The one surprise was that long multi-point strokes are smoothed into splines. My closed frame line and the tabard's outline ran away as loose loops through the border and round his feet, and I had to paint them out with border red and gold and redraw them as separate straight segments. The figure is deliberately flat, as the form requires. Next time I would give the face-plate and tabard the icon's stepped highlights (a few tonal steps laid as flat shapes over the base colour), draw the sea of fire as tighter stylised tongues rather than long lines, and spend more care on the angels.
