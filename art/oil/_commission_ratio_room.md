**Session: `ratio_room`** — **Where Ratio Lives** (concept).

Ratio has no physical form, but she has a room — the virtual space her
avatar inhabits when she talks to the Commando through a terminal: a
small, cosy, slightly wrong late-90s 3D room (Dreamcast-era low-poly:
flat-shaded walls, a checkered floor with a vanishing grid, a too-large
window showing a pixelated sky with a sun that is just a yellow octagon,
a beanbag, a bookshelf whose books are flat-coloured slabs, a potted plant
with triangle leaves, a CRT monitor on a low table showing — very small —
the Commando's armoured face looking in). Ratio's avatar (chibi, pale,
long black hair in faceted locks) sits on the beanbag hugging her knees,
turned toward the CRT, mid-sentence. Soft lighting with hard low-poly
facets; a faint scanline and fog at the room's far edge where the world
stops. Charming and lonely. 4:3. Deadline 04:40.
