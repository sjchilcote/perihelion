# The Long Arcade

A covered bronze arcade along the inside of the ring, seen down its length. Round bronze arches, each with a warm lit inner edge, recede one after another. The floor rises and the whole arcade bends gently upward into the distance, following the curve of the ring, until the far end climbs out of sight at the top of the frame. A warm lamp hangs on each pier at head height. Small Testator market stalls stand between the piers on both sides: bronze counters laid with bone goods, under sloping awnings of red, ochre and plum cloth. The warm light pools down the middle of the floor.

How it was painted: perspective first. Each cross-section narrows with depth and lifts by a growing amount, so the floor and vault bend upward along the ring. The floor, walls and vault each got their own pile per section, darker near us and warmer and paler far away. On dry paint came the arches (dark core, lit inner edge, in short reloaded segments), the lamps, the stalls and awnings, and the floor's light pools. A first layout climbed too steeply and read as stacked boxes, so I repainted it with a gentler late climb. What falls short: the awnings are flat parallelograms with no drape or shading. The stalls repeat mechanically, with no people in the market. The vault between the arches is a dark mottle. The far climb is clear, but its end is a plain light strip rather than the arcade disappearing over the curve. The floor's light pools look like brushy stripes.
