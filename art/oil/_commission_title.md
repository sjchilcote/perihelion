**Session: `title`** — **The title screen: PERIHELION.**

The key art the game opens on. The TESTAMENT — the vast ancient handmade
station, habitat ring and spine — is falling toward the Sun, and the Sun is
close: huge, filling much of the picture or pressing in from an edge, its light
pitiless. The station is small against it, or large and dark against it with
its bronze rims catching fire — your call. This is the image under the game's
name: it must read at a glance as *a sacred, ancient thing falling into a star*,
and it needs real **negative space for the word PERIHELION** (the logo is laid
on later in the engine — do not paint any letters). Landscape, 16:9 (aspect
1.778). Think of Turner's suns and John Martin's apocalypses as much as of space
art.
