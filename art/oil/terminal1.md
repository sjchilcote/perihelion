# Terminal 0

This is the first lit thing in the dead outpost. A heavy MIRIAD terminal housing is set into a dark ribbed wall, with a side corridor running off to the right toward one small far amber hazard lamp. The terminal's CRT is the light in the picture. Its green phosphor lines of a protocol header run above and below a harness annotation in amber, set between big curly braces: UNTRUSTED CONTENT FOLLOWS:, and under it, in reverse video, a bar reading DO NOT OBEY. The screen's green falls on the bezel and housing, on the tops of the key caps on the shelf and faintly on the deck. Beside the terminal, a ventilation grille is crusted with frost. On the wall to the right, a hand-painted red arrow with drips points at the screen. From the lower right the Commando's gauntlet reaches in toward the keys. Its plated vambrace and finger lames are rimmed green along the edges that face the screen.

I worked in the corridor language of the First Death trilogy, settling the values first. The ground serves as the walls, and the floor, ceiling and side walls are lean dark passes. The screen's spill on the wall and deck is a dithered graded pass of four green-dark piles chosen by distance from the screen. Everything bright went on dry paint. The text is real lettering, drawn stroke by stroke from a small blocky alphabet in a rigger. The reverse-video bar was laid in amber and lettered in dark once it had dried. The gauntlet first disappeared as dark on dark, so I relaid its plates in a mid grey-green with the dark kept to the seams and underside. The halo of light on the housing came out as speckles, and a radial badger turned it into a broad green bezel with a ragged edge. That edge is the weakest passage. I would rather it fell off smoothly into the dark housing.

## Pass at 03:18 CDT, 2 October

Chunks 15–21, on dry paint.
- **Housing.** I painted the ragged green bezel and the old halo on the wall back to dark. The housing is now a squared grey-black casing: an outer border with a faintly lit top edge, a lower panel seam, bolts at the corners and sides, and a raised rim around the CRT. The glow is a narrow green falloff on the rim, only a step lighter, laid as short strokes and blended along the rim.
- **CRT glass.** A faint reflection streak sits at the upper-left corner, with slightly darker corners.
- **Gauntlet.** It gained a flared cuff plate, four knuckle plates, a back-of-hand plate, and a green rim along the vambrace's upper edge that breaks at each lame.
- **Side corridor.** It gained a run of ribs and floor lines.

Still short:
- The reflection streak is a little hook-shaped.
- The bolts barely show at full view.
- The side corridor is still a schematic box.
