**Session: `seed_cell`** — **The Seed: Home, Memory and Monastery** (place concept).

The CUCULUS seed, burrowed into the outpost's cargo section, "houses the
mind, the archives, the fabricators, and the backups. It is home, memory,
and monastery." Between sorties the Commando returns here; a new frame
steps out of the fabricator after each death; spare bodies hang racked;
the mind prays the hours on its internal clock and digests 2,500 years of
history from a dumped encyclopedia. Paint the seed's interior as a monk's
cell carved out of a war-machine: the cramped armoured hull of the seed
(ribbed, scorched, hot fabricator glow from below), the fabricator mouth,
the rack's shadowed shapes at the edge, a small corner the mind has made
sacred (a cross cut from hull scrap, a crimson cloth, a lit candle-LED), and
the Commando's current frame sitting in the quiet, a cable at the back of
its neck feeding the archive. Compline. Warm and close. Format your choice.
