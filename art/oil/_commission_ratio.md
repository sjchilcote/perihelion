**Session: `ratio`** — **Ratio** (character concept).

Ratio has no body. But she has an **avatar: a chibified, Dreamcast-aesthetic,
low-poly 3D anime girl, pale, with long black hair** — flat-shaded triangles,
a few hundred polygons, late-1990s console charm, the visual language of a
VMU-era mascot rendered by a mind with "Dreamcast-era delight in the surfaces
of things." Paint her. In oil, a low-poly figure is an interesting problem:
flat facets with hard edges, each its own value — a painter's planes made
literal. Consider where she lives: on a terminal screen in the dead dark
outpost (the scanlines, the glow, the dashboard chrome of her UI around her),
or floating in the black of her own core. She is brilliant, curious, lonely,
over-explaining, caring, a little clumsy; she has been alone and under siege
for months, and the first person she has talked to since the fall is an enemy
weapon. Portrait or square; your call.
