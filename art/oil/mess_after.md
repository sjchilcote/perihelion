# Nighthawks, After

The pair to *Nighthawks at Miriad-7*: the same view from the dark corridor
through the mess's long window, with the same planes and mullions, after
the fall. The fluorescent tubes are dead. One still lies on the ceiling,
and the next has dropped at one end and hangs on its wire, slanting down
across the middle pane. The mess is dark. The only light is the corridor's
red emergency strip along the foot of the near wall. Its glow pools on the
corridor floor and falls the wrong way, in through the window, lying in a
dim red wedge across the counter front and up the back wall. Frost rimes
the edges of the glass and the mullions. The counter is bare except for
three mugs, standing where the three crew sat. One stool still stands,
red-lit. The other lies knocked over on the floor beyond, its post catching
the red. In the viewport on the corridor wall the Testament's faint bronze
arc still shows against the black. No one is there.

## How it was painted

I made it in 8 chunks on a dark ground at 16:9. It reuses the other
painter's window, ceiling, back-wall, counter and viewport shapes and the
mullion positions, so the two hang as a pair. A few things went wrong:
- My first darks were too light, a slate blue and a pale teal, and the
  red was orange and hard. On dry paint I repainted everything as graded
  near-blacks, with the red only where the strip's light could reach.
- The frost went wrong twice. A heavy stipple made bright white bands
  round every pane. Painting over it with a grey haze turned the bands into
  thick grey matting. In the end I repainted the band with the mess's own
  piles, kept only a thin dim rime at the very edge, and redrew the frame.
- The left dead tube was lost in that repaint and had to be put back.

Still rough: the frame reads grey rather than black. The back wall's red
patch is blotchy. The near corridor floor is a plain dark field. The
fallen stool is small.
