# After Perihelion

This is the pair to *Perihelion*, in the same coordinates and composition, hours later, past the Sun and climbing away. The corona is behind them now. All that is left of it is a few sparse, quiet ember streamers trailing in from the left edge, and the sky is black again with stars returning. The sail stands where it stood, scorched to a dull bronze-brown and torn by great rents with stars showing through. Shreds of film hang into the rents, their lips still faintly red, and the rim glows a dull cooling red in broken runs. In its lee the TESTAMENT is intact: the spine, the ring nearly edge-on, the maw, and the four tethers to the sail. It is a near-black silhouette, with a thin warm rim on the edges facing the receding Sun, a few dull red dashes where the forward edges are still cooling, and a scatter of ring windows lit warm again. Shed plates drift slowly around it, a few glinting, with tiny embers among them. The mood is stillness after violence.

**How it was painted.** It took 18 chunks on a blue-black ground, reusing the spine, ring, maw, sail and tether coordinates from *Perihelion*. Two mistakes cost time. My first warm glow for the receding corona read as a planet, and the black cover over it left a hard slab and a stripe, which had to be knocked out. A brown "bronze" station then read lighter than the sky and had to be repainted near-black, with only rims and windows for light. The rents first had scorch halos that read as donuts; I broke them back into the film and hung shreds across them.

**Still short:** the sail's face is a little lighter and browner than I wanted, so the torn shield reads less dark than in *Perihelion*. The streamers at the left edge stop rather abruptly. The drifting plates are small at full view.
