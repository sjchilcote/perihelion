# The Teacher

Years later, on the trust path. A small warm schoolroom, lit by one hanging lamp: its light lies in a soft glow on the back wall and a pool on the floor, and the corners fall into warm dark. On the wall hangs a dark slate in a bronze frame, and on it the docent has chalked a big fold-notation glyph. It is a pentagon room holding a smaller copy of itself, turned, holding a smaller one turned again. Notches on two of its edges name its doorways. A gluing arc runs from it to a small square room, and a self-gluing arc leaves its lower edge and comes back in at its side. The docent stands at the slate's right in its bronze robe, bone face turned to the board, arm raised with the chalk at the small room's corner, and the lamp lights its hood and shoulders. On a low bench in front sit three MIRIAD children in small grey coveralls, their backs to us, each with a little bone tablet tilted on their knees and a tiny copied glyph on it. The middle one has a hand up. In the doorway at the right, dark against the grey MIRIAD corridor beyond, the Commando stands still, listening.

**How I painted it.** Light structure first: the wall in three soft steps around the lamp, the floor pool, the slate and the door. The chalk went onto the dry slate edge by edge, each edge a separate stroke, so the polygons keep their corners. Docent, children and Commando went in as solid masks, then on set paint the lamp's light on the crowns, shoulders and room-facing edges. The Commando, laid over the still-wet door, first came out translucent and was restated opaque. Still short: the wall glow reads as a painted oval, the children's coveralls are flat, and the Commando's warm edge is faint.
