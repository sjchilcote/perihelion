**Session: `impact`** — **Impact** (intro cinematic beat; companion to "Last Minutes of the Approach").

The beat between the approach and the cold open: the CUCULUS strikes. A
seed-projectile fired like a bullet across a solar system, months in flight,
hits the dead MIRIAD research outpost (a small, gray, right-angled prefab
cluster with amber hazard markings, bolted to the flank of the vast ancient
bronze TESTAMENT) and burrows into its cargo section. Seen from outside, in
vacuum: no fireball billowing in air — a flash, a spray of incandescent
debris and torn panels flung out in straight lines, venting atmosphere
freezing to a glittering plume, the outpost's skin buckling around the entry
wound. The TESTAMENT's worn bronze scale-plates and rows of tiny windows tower
behind or beside it, utterly indifferent, lit hard by an off-frame Sun. The
director's favorite painting so far is the approach shot: hard raking
sunlight, everything else in black, a structure cropped by the frame so it is
bigger than the picture, scale sold by tiny lit windows. Carry that language
into this moment. Landscape, 16:9.
