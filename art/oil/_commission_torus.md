**Session: `torus`** — **Hydroponics Torus** (a place concept).

HYDROPONICS TORUS, in the MIRIAD outpost: a ring-shaped garden deck where the
research crew grew their food under lamps — racks of plant beds in long trays,
irrigation pipes, grow-lights, MIRIAD's right angles and modular parts, amber
hazard marks. Now the power has failed: the plants have died or gone feral
in the dark (pale etiolated stems straining toward one surviving grow-lamp,
or toward a viewport full of Sun), frost on the trays where coolant leaked.
The geometry: walk half the arc and you have walked a full loop; you come
back rotated. Show the torus floor curving UP and away in both directions
(a ring seen from inside), so that the far end of the garden hangs overhead —
and one detail for the attentive eye that is turned the wrong way (the same
bed, the same dropped tool, seen twice, rotated). Make it beautiful and
melancholy: the crew's garden, the only living green in the outpost, dying
toward the light. Format your choice.
