**Session: `warden`** — **A warden at its post** (character/enemy concept art).

The TESTAMENT's corrupted turrets and defenders ARE Testators: the lowest
caste, sub-sentient handmade machines still at their posts after a million
years, no longer reliably able to tell friend from foe. Paint one of them:
a single defender, an individual (no two were ever made alike), in its niche
or at a gate inside the station. It carries the full Testator language —
worn bronze, verdigris, bone inlay, golden-angle ornament, the faint hand of
a particular maker, edges rounded smooth by eons of cleaning — and over it
the corruption: a defacement of the elegy, never a replacement of it (the
Adversary's touch is inconsistency itself: something on it that won't hold
still, that doesn't agree with the rest). It should be beautiful, dangerous
and pitiable at once — "a person-shaped absence when destroyed." Show enough
of its form that an artist could build the sprite from it; the setting can
be dim and close. Format your choice.
