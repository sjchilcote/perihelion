# The Wanderer above the Sun, Unfolded

This is the pair to *The Wanderer above the Sun*: the same portrait canvas, the same crown of the Testament's bronze dome, the same vast Sun rising behind the hull. Standing where the pilgrim stood is what the frame became on the apostasy path. It is seen from behind, and it fills the Sun. A broad, humped carapace stands on eight jointed legs. The old soldier's torso and helm still rise from its front, with a rag of the crimson capelet caught on its shoulders and the bone cross worn to a ghost. In the first painting the Sun made a halo of his helmet. Here that halo is broken by antler-like sensor masts that branch up through the disc. Cold blue light from the old cross visor leaks around the edges of the helm. Slung beneath the carapace is a cage of dark bars holding violet Adversary tissue, with pale eyes looking out. Where he once cast two shadows and the shadow of a rifle, eight long leg-shadows now rake down the scales toward us. It is the same sublime, curdled.

How it was painted: The work was big and simple because of the time. The sky and Sun are concentric bands of opaque paint, running from a near-white lemon core through cadmium and orange to red earth and a violet-black. The hull is bronze bands with rows of scale lips, each a dark arc with a lit lip above it. The creature is a single bone black and ultramarine silhouette with jointed round-brush legs, and its antlers were drawn by a small recursive branching stroke. The paint surprised me twice. My first silhouette, laid into the still-wet Sun, picked up the yellow and went grey. I let it dry and repainted it at one and a half times the size, much darker, and the first small carapace that showed through became the slung, caged graft. Also, my opening chunk (all the sky bands plus the hull) took almost ten minutes to compute and nearly ran out of time. With more time I would give the Sun a crisp disc edge as in the original, make the eyes in the graft larger, and model the carapace's plates instead of leaving it a flat silhouette.
