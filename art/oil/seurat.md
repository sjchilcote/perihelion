# A Sunday on the Ring

After Seurat's "A Sunday Afternoon on the Island of La Grande Jatte". It is a Sunday on the TESTAMENT's ring a million years ago, while the Testators lived. A long lawn runs along the inside of the ring, and in the distance the ring's floor curves up into a pale blue sky. At the left lies a blue strip of water with a small white sail. Testators stand in stiff, calm profile across the lawn: bell-robed figures in bronze and deep violet-blue with long pale skulls, each throwing a long flat cast shadow. In the right foreground a tall pair stands in profile. The nearer one holds a coral parasol and a leash, and on the leash is a small jointed creature, like Seurat's monkey. Another reclines in the deep shadow at the lower left. Smaller figures stand and sit at different depths, one with a white parasol, two seated by the water, and thin bone docents wait in the distance near the ring. Two great shadows lie across the lawn. Everything is built of small dots of pure colour: cerulean, cobalt, violet and lemon in the sky; lemon-green, yellow, emerald and orange on the lit lawn; ultramarine, viridian, violet and orange in the shadows; orange, sienna and blue on the figures.

How it was painted: 8 chunks, 3:2. I laid flat underpainted zones first (sky, ring band, water, lawn, shadows), then stippled each zone with three or four pure-colour piles in small dots, and the stippling was fast. The figures are flat bell-robed silhouettes laid opaque and then stippled with their own dots. One bug wasted a chunk: `above()` given a point list made a full-canvas mask, so the first lay-in was all sky. Using a function worked. Clearing the first blobby heads left flat discs, which I re-stippled with each zone's dots. Still short: the heads are cream lumps rather than precise long skulls. The two foreground figures are big, flat and overlapping, and too much like bottles. The sky has a visible step. Seurat's figures are far more exactly drawn.
