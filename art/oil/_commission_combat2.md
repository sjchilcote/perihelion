**Session: `combat2`** — **A combat shot: the lightning cannon (second attempt).**

The director's description, verbatim:

> the Commando running backwards down a corridor, cable trailing from the
> lightning cannon, the first pilot sparks jumping from the projectile to a
> Warden chasing him, flanked by smaller enemies, as the cable begins to burst
> in to plasma.

A first painter attempted this (look at /tmp/cp/combat/final.png and read
/tmp/cp/combat/ABOUT.md). What to do differently: the figures there read as
flat cut-outs, the backward run doesn't read, and the bursting cable — the
subject — is small. So:
- **Camera** low, close and tilted, from in front of the Commando and slightly
  to the side, so we see him running BACKWARDS toward us/the camera's side:
  torso twisted, one leg mid-stride behind him, cannon braced forward at the
  hip, weight falling back. Motion in every line.
- **The cable is the hero:** a long whip from the cannon muzzle out across the
  corridor to the dart, which is just reaching the Warden. Along its length
  it is bursting into plasma — white-hot core, violet-blue sheath, beads and
  ruptures — biggest near the dart, still a cable near the muzzle.
- **The pilot sparks:** thin jagged feelers leaping from the dart to the
  Warden's bronze — the first instant of the discharge.
- **The Warden**: huge, handmade Testator bronze and bone, golden-angle
  ornament, corrupted (something on it that won't hold still), bearing down,
  filling the far half of the corridor; smaller enemies flanking it (husks:
  subverted outpost machines; mites: fast swarm). Rough is fine; they are
  supporting cast.
- **Light**: the discharge is the only light — hard, cold-white, throwing
  long shadows from everything toward the viewer; the corridor otherwise
  black. Model the forms with that light (rim light that wraps, core shadow).
Landscape, 16:9 or wider. It's 21:58; **deadline 22:45**. Keep the plan
simple and big; spend the time on the cable and the figure.
