**Session: `core_ratio`** — **Where Ratio Lives** (place concept).

Ratio has no body, but she has a place: the outpost's **hardened core**, the
vault she retreated into when the outpost fell, cut off from most of her own
peripherals, alone, blind and under siege for months. MIRIAD utility
architecture (right angles, grids, identical modules, cool grays, amber
hazard), but this is the one room in the outpost that still has power: racks
of server modules in a sealed armoured cylinder, status LEDs in long rows
(most dark, a few still green or amber), frost on the outer shell, scorch and
claw marks on the blast door from the things that tried to get in, and
something of her inside — a small lit display on a maintenance terminal
showing her low-poly avatar, or her name, or a ledger she keeps to herself
("DAYS ALONE"). Lonely, warm-in-the-cold, the last lit window in a dead
building. Format your choice. It's 22:30; **deadline 22:55** — keep it big,
simple and finished.
