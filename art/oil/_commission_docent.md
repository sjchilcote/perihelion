**Session: `docent`** — **A Docent** (character concept).

The Testator docents are the station's automated teaching systems: ancient,
courteous, damaged, speaking in a translation the station derives on the
fly — "the closest thing the Testators have to a voice." Handmade (no two
alike): bronze, bone, golden-angle ornament, worn smooth by a million years.
A docent need not be humanoid (one painter made a cracked bronze bell with a
sunflower head; look at /tmp/cp/museum/final.png). Paint a portrait of one
docent, close, as you would paint a saint or a scholar: what it holds (a
lesson plate? a lamp?), how it has been repaired, where it is damaged, the
fold-notation inscribed on it, the courtesy in its posture. Warm light,
dark ground. Portrait format. It's 22:30; **deadline 22:55** — keep it big,
simple and finished.
