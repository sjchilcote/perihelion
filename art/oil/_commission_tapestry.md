**Session: `tapestry`** — **The Perihelion Tapestry** (after the Bayeux Tapestry).

The Bayeux Tapestry: a long narrative strip of embroidered scenes on
linen, flat figures in a few wool colours (terracotta, ochre, green,
blue-black), decorative borders above and below with little beasts, the
scenes flowing into one another with a few architectural dividers.
Subject: the whole story of the game in one strip, read left to right:
the CUCULUS crossing to the station; the seed burrowing; the first frame
dying and the second taking up the tabard; the dead outpost; Ratio on a
screen; docents in their museum; Wardens fighting; the sail spreading;
perihelion; the station sailing on. Flat embroidered look: stitched-line
contours, wool colours, linen ground, little beasts and fold-glyphs in
the borders, a band of abstract lettering above. Very wide (3:1).
