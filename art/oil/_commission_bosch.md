**Session: `bosch`** — **The Garden of Folding Delights** (a Bosch study).

A sixteenth master: Hieronymus Bosch, the right panel of "The Garden of
Earthly Delights" (Hell) — a night landscape crowded with small,
precise, impossible creatures and structures, fires on the horizon, the
"tree-man" — painted with jewel-like clarity. Subject: the Adversary's
space as Bosch would have imagined it: a dark landscape where Testator
architecture is being folded into impossible shapes, small precise
creatures (Wardens, scuttlers, a Shrieker-bell) going about strange
business, a giant hollow "tree-man" made of a folded bronze station
section, fires of cold violet on the horizon, tiny human figures (MIRIAD
crew) caught in it. Many small crisp details, each made of a few
strokes. Portrait 3:4. Deadline 06:20.
