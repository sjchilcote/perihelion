# The Vigil

Key art for the endless holdout mode. We look across the heart of a Testator fold. Five petal-shaped doorways stand in an arc in the dark bronze wall, separated by bronze ribs. Each glows a cold blue, and inside each one is the same doorway again, smaller, with the same doorway again inside that: every route adds a room that was already there. Through them come splinters of the Adversary, dark slabs with cyan and magenta edges that won't keep a consistent shape, and on the far left a corrupted Testator defender with one bronze eye. The Commando is a dark silhouette braced on the worn ring of the dais, his back to us and his rifle raised at the left doorway. A tracer runs from the muzzle to the Testator's eye. The muzzle flash is the only warm light in the room. It gives him hot rims on one side, the doorways give him cold rims on the other, and it throws an orange pool around his feet full of decades of spent casings and scorch streaks. A crimson tabard with a pale cross hangs at his waist.

How it was painted: 8 chunks in about ten minutes of painting, kept to big shapes as the brief asked. I laid opaque bone-black and sienna darks first, then the doors as nested clipped petal shapes, then the silhouettes, then the rims and the flash after a day's drying. Two things went wrong. Even after drying, the dark bronze piles over the bright ribs came out lighter than intended, so the ribs are still a little loud. And the band where each doorway's glow darkens toward the top stayed hard after blending. With more time I would break up the flat floor pool, model the figure's armour beyond its rims, and make the things in the doorways more varied and less geometric.

## Second pass

Chunks 9–12. I knocked the ribs down to an opaque near-black bronze with one dull lip and a thin cold edge. The hard band in each doorway's glow got a middle blue laid across the seam, blended inside the door only; it is softer now but still faintly visible. The Adversary is no longer slabs. Out of the doorways come tapering reaching tongues aimed at the heart, each painted twice slightly out of register: a cyan outline on one body and a magenta outline on a second that doesn't line up with it. Their roots are knots, two overlapping bodies that don't agree, with nested folds inside each that open into smaller copies of the same shape, and a pale point of light where they open. The Commando is rebuilt as armour plates. Each plate is dark gunmetal with a black thickness edge, a hot muzzle-flash edge on the upper left and a cold doorway edge on the right, and his far side is turned cold and dark. The rear leg is braced back and the torso set over it to take the recoil, with the rifle restated long and heavy. The plates came out more olive than steel, and the stance is still stiffer than I'd want.
