**Session: `tesseract`** — **Tesseract Deck** (a place concept).

TESSERACT DECK: sixteen identical rooms glued on a 4-cube — "pure graph."
Every room has four doors and every door leads to one of the other rooms, so
that from any doorway you can see, nested through the next doorway and the
next, the same room again — and yourself, from behind or from the side, in
rooms that are each one step around a different axis. In the Testators'
depths (bronze and bone, embodiment abandoned: no furniture, ornament is pure
fold-notation — see /tmp/cp/notes/fold_notation.md; each room's floor carries
its 4-bit address as notation, and the address differs by one mark through
each door). Paint the view through a doorway into the deck: the same room
receding through doorways in two or three directions at once, each step
subtly turned, a small figure (the Commando) visible several times at
different turns. Cold, exact, vertiginous. It's 21:57; plan for ~45 minutes.
Format your choice.
