# Holy Ratio

The pair to *Saint Palmer*: Ratio's avatar as a saint of the Hodegetria type, the one who shows the way. She stands frontal on the same flat gold ground inside the same dark red border, chibi and solemn. A big pale face is framed by flat black locks falling to her waist, with her bangs cut in points. Her big dark eyes carry square white highlights, and she has a small mouth and a touch of blush. Her large gold halo has incised rays and, faintly, the horizontal scanlines of a screen. Her robe is flat deep blue with gold assist lines, a gold neckline and a gold hem. Where a Hodegetria holds the Child, she holds before her a small cream-cased CRT. On it, in green phosphor and scanlines, is the Commando's helm with its single bright optic, and short rays of green glow come off the screen. Above, in the segment of blue heaven, is a pixel sun: a yellow octagon with square rays. Below her feet, small, the seed rides as a little ark on the same sea of fire, a pale pod with a red cross in a bronze boat. Red abstract glyphs stand on either side of the halo. It is meant to be a little funny and entirely sincere: the mind who is all speech, made into an image of devotion holding the image of the soldier.

**How it was painted.** The same icon method as Saint Palmer: flat body shapes first, then all the drawing as lines on dry paint, and this time every outline drawn as short straight segments or circle arcs, so nothing could loop away. One surprise. Her face, hands and the CRT casing were laid while the black hair and blue robe under them were still wet, and came up a speckled grey. I restated them heavier once everything was dry, then put the black bangs and eyes back over the dry skin. With the darks laid first and left to dry, the pale shapes came out clean. Next time I'd paint the pale shapes into reserved gaps rather than over the darks, give the sea of fire tighter flame tongues than these dashes, and add a few stepped highlights to the robe.
