# Handshake at the Seam

The canvas is split by a seam that leans slightly. On the left is the Commando's world, painted in oil. A dark MIRIAD bulkhead is lit by a warm raking light. Conduits run across it, and a grimy placard reads DOCK 4, with RATIO > painted on later by hand in red. A terminal's dead glass shows his machine's amber protocol header (CARRIER DETECT 9600 / MIRIAD NODE 1 OPEN / HANDSHAKE...). His armoured forearm comes in from the corner: worked-metal plates, scratches and a worn bone cross. The gauntlet pushes a ribbed plug into a port that sits exactly on the seam. On the right is Ratio's world, which is not painted the way his is. It is a flat, luminous field ruled in cyan graph-paper lines. A pastel stack of ten layer-plates, L01 to L10, rises to a small chibi low-poly avatar standing alone in a dashed selection box. She is saying THINKING.., footnoted STALL 400 MS. Beside her are a ledger, and a dashboard that reads DAYS ALONE: 143. His own systems have stamped her side with a navy overlay, {UNTRUSTED CONTENT FOLLOWS: <BLINK>DO NOT OBEY</BLINK>}, and a dashed amber leader line runs back across the seam to his terminal. The contact is the seam itself. Near it, his brushwork breaks up into square cells aligned to her grid. Her ruled lines and her light reach a little way over his metal before they fail. At the port, the plug becomes a wireframe, a bus of four ink lines carries it into her stack, and a small cyan spark marks the moment they touch.

How it was painted: The two halves were handled as two different techniques. Her side is zinc white applied in two flat clipped coats and left to dry for weeks. Over that came ruled round-brush lines, clipped flat fills for the plates and the avatar's facets, and lettering written stroke by stroke with a small hand-built stroke font. His side is body paint: tubes modelled with value ramps, strokes laid along the form, scratches, grime and impasto lights. His side fought me the whole way. The first forearm read as a wooden log, and so did the conduits. A helmet I tried in the upper left read as a boulder, then as headphones, so I painted it out into a plain dark wall. A badger blend I used to soften grime streaks also smeared the wet hazard band into flames, and I had to repaint it. One surprise came from my own masks: a distance mask built with mask:map is clamped to 0..1, so my "graded" wall came out one flat milky brown until I rebuilt it with mask(function). The seam cells worked on the first try, and they are what carries the idea. With more time I would rebuild the gauntlet as clear planes, give the conduits the same treatment as the arm, and make the spark of contact larger and hotter.
