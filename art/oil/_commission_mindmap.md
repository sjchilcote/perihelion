**Session: `mindmap`** — **The Mind Map** (UI / concept art).

Between deaths, the Commando digests 2,500 years of missed history on a
**mind map**: "a graph of concepts representing the Commando digesting
2,500 years of missed history, the station's revelations, and his own
situation — integrating (or failing to integrate) all of it into his
worldview." Its root nodes expand out from what the seed carried: "a copy
of the public encyclopedia — Catholic Space Robot Microsoft Encarta" —
plus what he learns on the station. Four regions: *Vocation* (faith: just-
war doctrine, the theology of his resurrection-loop, the Testators as
fellow servants), *History* (the long peace, the retrieval, the civil
wars), *Comprehension* (connection-space theory, Testator engineering,
Adversary biology), *The Void* (Adversary organ integration, discarding
wartime constraint kernels, the arithmetic of futility). Paint it as a
devotional object the way a Church war-machine would see its own mind: an
illuminated-manuscript tree or rose window of nodes and edges — gold and
crimson for Vocation, sober inks for History, cold geometric blue for
Comprehension, and creeping into one quadrant an unfolding violet dark for
The Void; unlit nodes in fog-of-war; the seed's encyclopedia at the root
like a bound book. Square. Deadline 01:35.
