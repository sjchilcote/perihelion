**Session: `mat_deck`** — **Floor material: MIRIAD deck plate.**

Read the rules below first; they override anything above about composition.

The outpost's floors: hasty prefab, right angles, grids, modular IDENTICAL
parts (MIRIAD standardizes: every plate the same size, every rivet row the
same), cool structural grays with a faint verdigris-blue cast, amber hazard
striping only as a rare accent (if at all — it will repeat everywhere), wear
that says refusal: scuffs, boot-polished paths, grime in the seams, rust
blooming at rivets, a mismatched replacement plate or two, patched with
welded strips. Treadplate (diamond pattern) panels mixed with smooth panels
is welcome. Make it work for corridors, storage bays, quarters and the
medbay alike: generic, but alive.
