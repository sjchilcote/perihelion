**Session: `choir`** — **The Choir of Docents** (place concept).

Somewhere in the TESTAMENT the docents gather, a hundred of them, to sing
the warning in fold-notation chant on the anniversary of the station's
founding — every year for a million years, to an empty hall. Paint the
choir: a tall bronze hall with tiers of choir-stalls rising on both sides
in perspective, each stall holding a docent (bone faces, single lenses,
bronze robes), all turned toward a great bone lectern at the far end
where an open tablet of fold notation stands lit by one shaft of light;
their lenses all catching that light, a hundred small cold points. The
Commando, small, has just come in at the near end and stopped. Warm
bronze, cold lens-glints, one shaft of pale light. Value structure first,
lean darks, lights last. Portrait or landscape. Deadline 03:25.
