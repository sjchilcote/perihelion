# The Garden of Folding Delights

After the Hell panel of Bosch's "Garden of Earthly Delights": the Adversary's space as a night landscape. Along the horizon, fires of cold violet lick up behind black silhouettes of Testator architecture being folded into impossible shapes: arches bent over, a tower kinked at the waist, a dome turned upside down, a ramp spiralling into its own hole, grey smoke rising in plumes. In the middle distance a folded town leans into itself, its windows lit violet, a bridge knotted into a loop, and a broken bronze ring stands on edge with figures climbing through it. In the centre stands the tree-man, made of a folded bronze station section: a broken-open egg of a body with a table of tiny crew inside it, a ladder up to it, two hollow pipe legs standing in boats in a dark pool, and a pale face turned back over its shoulder under a wide bronze disc. On the disc two figures dance round a folded conduit like Bosch's bagpipe. A procession of crew and pale souls files toward it behind a Warden. Figures wade in a ring in the pool, one upside down, and a red banner stands in the water. In the foreground, scuttlers carry a pale figure on a folded arch, a Shrieker-bell on legs traps someone beneath it while violet rings of its cry radiate out, a second Warden carries a rider, a pale creature with a plate for a head walks a beast on a leash, and a twisted bronze arch rises at the lower left. Tiny violet eyes glow in the dark.

How it was painted: 5 chunks, 3:4. The sky, the fire band (blended wet) and the land are broad flat passes. Everything else is small flat shapes and single precise strokes laid on dry paint. The first figures were too small to see at full view and had to be restated at about 2.5× the size. Still short of Bosch: the ground is flat brown with large empty stretches where Bosch fills every inch; the tree-man is a flat yellow cut-out rather than Bosch's pale, modelled eggshell; the fires are thin lines rather than glowing blazes; and the detail is sparse.
