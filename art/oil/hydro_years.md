# Hydroponics, Years Later

The fourth of the hydroponics set (before, falling, after, and now this), with
the same geometry as *Hydroponics, Before the Fall* and *The Night It Fell*.
It is the trust ending, years on. The grow-lamps burn pink-white again over
beds that are green again but planted differently. Among the crops stand
Testator bone-flowers: ivory heads ringed with fine petals, their seeds set
in golden-angle spirals. Down the middle of the floor, through every bed and
up and over onto the floor overhead, runs a narrow bronze channel the
docents made by hand. Its water glints, and its rims are studded at
phyllotactic intervals. Above the viewport the seven crew badges from the
Requiem hang in a row, each on a short cord from a red pin. The printed
placard on the right wall is covered now with children's drawings: a sun, a
stick figure, a flower drawn as a spiral. A child's small blue coverall,
with its reflective band and amber patch, hangs over the handle of the work
cart, which still carries its flat of seedlings. The yellow watering can
stands upright by the beds. No one is in sight. Warm.

## How it was painted

I made it in 15 chunks at 1.6:1. I replayed my own Hydroponics, Before the
Fall chunks for the room, beds, crop, tray lips, hazard strips, window and
lamps, skipping the ones whose lamp glows had failed. Then I painted the
new life on dry paint:
- the bronze channel, with its water, beaten rims and spiral-spaced studs;
- the bone-flowers;
- the badges, the drawings, the coverall and the can.
The bone-flowers first went on as flat ochre discs, with their spirals lost
in the same colour. I found every head again by comparing images from
before and after that pass, then repainted each one ivory, with a dark seed
spiral and a ring of petals.

Still rough: the lamp glow is the blotchy pink scumble carried over from
the replay. The badges are small for the weight they carry. The child's
coverall is tiny against the cart.
