**Session: `sunrise`** — **Perihelion** (a loose, abstract Turner study).

A study in the manner of late Turner (e.g. "Norham Castle, Sunrise", "Light
and Colour"): the TESTAMENT falling into the Sun, dissolved almost entirely
into light and colour — a dark bronze smudge of the station at the edge of a
blazing vortex of whites, yellows and pale golds, the corona swirling, a few
hard notes (the ring's arc, one warm window) to hold it together. No
detail; all atmosphere. Turner worked thin over a white ground with broad
wet washes and scraped lights; here: a pale warm ground, big glazes and
scumbles of yellows and whites, everything blended wet in sweeping curves,
then a few crisp darks and lights at the end, once dry. Loose and fast is
right for this one: 15–25 chunks. Landscape. Deadline 02:00.
