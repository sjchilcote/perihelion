**Session: `cockpit`** — **Final Approach, from the Cockpit** (POV concept).

The Commando's view from inside the CUCULUS on final approach (pair to
the director's favourite, "Last Minutes of the Approach",
/tmp/cp/approach/final.png): the cramped dark cockpit framing the picture
— canopy struts, a worn crimson strip of cloth tied to one strut, a small
bone cross hanging and swinging, instrument panels glowing amber below —
and through the canopy glass the TESTAMENT filling the view, huge and
close, bronze scale-plates and the ring's curve lit by the Sun from
behind and to the side, the target hull-patch marked by a cold cyan HUD
reticle projected on the glass with a few angular glyphs and a closing
range number. The Sun's glare at one edge flaring across the scratched
glass. Value structure: dark cockpit frame, bright station. Lights last
on dry paint. 16:9. Deadline 04:35.
