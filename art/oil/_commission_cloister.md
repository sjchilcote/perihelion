**Session: `cloister`** — **The Cloister** (concept art for a game place).

"The Cloister, a safe room folded into the station's blind spots, is where
you go when the hole isn't enough." In the game it is a sanctuary the
Commando can retreat into: a room the station's geometry hides — it has
no place in any floor plan; it is folded into the gaps between other rooms,
reachable only through the station's own impossible joins. Paint it as the
one place in the TESTAMENT where nothing hunts him: a cloister in the
monastic sense (a covered walk around an enclosed garth — but which way is
"around" in a folded space? perhaps the arcade's four sides are glued so that
it has only three, or five; perhaps the garth's light comes from a sun that
isn't the Sun). Testator handcraft (bronze, bone, golden-angle ornament,
fold-notation — see /tmp/cp/notes/fold_notation.md), the kind of
hospitality that was computed rather than remembered, and maybe a trace of
the Commando having made it his own: a cleared corner, a prayer niche, a
spare frame, a scrap-metal cross. Peace after so much dread; stillness;
the hours prayed by an internal clock. Format your choice.
