**Session: `vigil`** — **The Long Vigil** (concept art for an ending).

The gentlest ending, and the saddest: "everything preserved, nothing saved,
nobody alone." The Commando has lost his faith; Ratio — the outpost's
surviving mind, who has no body, only a voice and a few lit instruments —
agreed with him, honest to the end. They kept the station together for
centuries: two lighthouse-keepers for a coastline with no more ships.
Now the TESTAMENT's orbit has finally given out, and they let it fall into
the Sun, and make no effort to leave. Paint the last evening of that watch:
perhaps the frame, old and much-repaired, sitting on the hull (or at a
window, or in the outpost's last lit room) beside one small warm light that
is Ratio; the Sun filling the sky ahead, close now, the hull's bronze
glowing in it; the half-built solar sail that was never needed, hanging
torn. Quiet, tender, final; dusk without a night after it. Format your
choice.
