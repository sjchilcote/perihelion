# The Great Wave off the Corona

After Hokusai. A solar prominence rears up off the Sun's limb like the Great Wave. It is a huge flat mass of vermilion-orange with a deep red core, banded inside its curl with gold and a cream rim, and outlined in dark ink. Its crest breaks into a row of claw-like cream fingers of plasma that curl forward and down, flinging drops into a flat blue sky. Beneath the curl, framed in the trough as Fuji is, stands the TESTAMENT, small and calm: a dark spine with its ring, sitting on the gold surface of the Sun. Flat orange swells roll across that surface like Hokusai's sea. Three slim cream CUCULUS sparks ride the wave's face like the boats. At the top left is a cream cartouche with columns of abstract glyph marks and a red seal.

How it was painted: 6 chunks, 3:2, on a pale cream ground. I laid flat masked colour fields with opaque body paint, which is the right use of masks for a print: sky, Sun, wave, then the wave's inner bands and the swells. On dry paint went the claws (hooked strokes with a pointed round, each finger reaching out from the crest and curling forward), the TESTAMENT, the sparks, ink outlines and flow lines. The box has no Prussian blue, so the "Prussian" sky is ultramarine and cerulean with a touch of bone black. It reads as a strong cobalt and is a little brighter than Hokusai's blue. My first claws were too small to read and had to be restated much bigger. A closed polyline for the cartouche frame curved wildly round the corners (the stroke smooths through its points), so I painted it out with sky and redrew the frame as four straight strokes. The ink line of the limb ran across the wave's base and needed covering. With more time I would add a few darker blue swells behind the TESTAMENT, a top-band bokashi to the sky, and finer flow lines inside the wave.
