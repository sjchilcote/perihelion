# Ratio and the Big Knight, page 3

The last spread of the picture book, in the same flat gouache shapes, warm palette, gutter and text lines as pages 1 and 2.

On the left page it is night. The three of them sit together on the curve of the gold ring under a black sky full of small stars. The sleepy purple bug is curled at the left, one eye still open. Tiny Ratio, in her white dress with her black hair down her back, turns her face up and points high with one arm. A dotted line runs from her finger to the one big warm star she has found. The big knight sits beside her, seen from behind: his rounded helm, his crimson tabard with the pale cross on his back, and his one dot of light turned to look where she points.

On the right page, under a peach sky, the three of them are small and far away, walking along the gold ring toward the big warm sun: Ratio in front, the knight beside her, the bug trotting behind on its little legs. Below them, a red ribbon with forked ends carries THE END in plain hand-drawn cream capitals.

How it was painted: page 1's lesson was applied before the first stroke. The gold ring and the sun went onto the bare cream ground first, and the black sky was painted around them, so they stayed clean yellow. The figures still caught it. The knight and the bug, laid onto the night sky, came out mottled, and Ratio's dress greyed where it met her wet hair. All of them dried for days and were repainted at heavy coverage, and the ribbon's lettering was restated after the red dried. Still weak: Ratio's dress keeps a faint grey veil on the left page, the right-page figures are small (Ratio about 50 units tall), and the knight's light-dot on the left page sits on the edge of his helm because he is seen from behind.
