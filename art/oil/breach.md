# Through the Breach

The Commando's frame takes his first step out of the TESTAMENT. Behind him, the last of the station fills the left of the picture: a torn wall of bronze plates in courses, warm where the station's light still falls on it, darker toward the ragged edge, with verdigris in a few seams. Its floor runs out under his heel and stops. Ahead is the Adversary's space in cold inverted colour: ice-blue, lime, magenta and violet where the station is gold and brown. The floor he steps onto runs on, folds up into a wall, rolls over into a ceiling that is tiled like a floor, and turns down again off the edge. A corridor floor loops round into itself with a half-twist. A room hangs in the dark, its outside faces turned up and to the right while the same face shows its inside walls converging into it. Loose floor slabs and bronze shards drift, and the shards change colour as they leave the wall: bronze, then violet, then cold blue. He is small, with a staff and his crimson cloth, warm light on his back and cold on his front, one cold optic. A faint skin of light, the only air there is, goes with him.

**How it was painted.** Landscape, toned in a cold indigo close to the final field, so the background needed only one body pass. I painted the impossible geometry as flat hinged planes of body colour, and they only read as floors once the paint had dried and I drew tile joints across them and laid bright lines on their hinges. Three things went wrong. I knew about two of them from the studio notes and still walked into them: a warm yellow glaze over the dark bronze went olive-green, and a dark glaze on bronze dried streaky. I repainted both opaquely. The third: my soft "lose the halo" passes round the room and the loop were blended flat and came out as pale discs, and they ran over the folding planes because my mask didn't exclude them. Covering them with the field's dark, carried across the whole upper band, turned them back into sky. I'd do it differently next time: build exact masks for every solid form first, so that no veil or blend touches them, and give the figure more drawing. He is nearly a pictogram.

## Pass at 01:42 CDT, Oct 2

Chunks 18–22, after a 13-minute replay. **The TESTAMENT side** is now worn bronze scale-plates instead of bricks: 100 plates with rounded lower edges, laid row by row from the bottom up so each row's lips overlap the tops of the row beneath. Each row is offset by the golden fraction, over a dark ground left showing in the gaps. Each plate has a lip lit warm by the light behind us (warmest low and left) and a dark shadow cast on the plate below. Plates are missing along the breach, and others hang loose and turned there, with the cold on their outer edges. A few have verdigris. **The figure** is redrawn as a frame leaning into the step: helm thrust forward with a cold optic, pauldron, breastplate, legs in stride, his arm out to a staff planted ahead, and crimson cloth falling and lifting behind him.
