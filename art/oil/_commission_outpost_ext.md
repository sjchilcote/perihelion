**Session: `outpost_ext`** — **The Outpost, from outside** (exterior concept).

The MIRIAD research outpost: "a hasty prefabricated research base bolted
onto the exterior of something much older and much larger." Paint it close
up from outside, in the hard raking sunlight of the director's favourite
painting (/tmp/cp/approach/final.png): a cluster of gray right-angled prefab
modules, trusses, antennae, dish arrays, amber hazard markings and
machine-language placards, clamped with crude human brackets onto the
curving worn-bronze scale-plates of the TESTAMENT's hull — the plates going
on far past the frame on every side. Dead: no lights in its windows, one
antenna bent, a scorched module where something got out. The scale contrast
is the subject: the outpost is the size of a few of the hull's plates.
Landscape. Deadline 01:00.
