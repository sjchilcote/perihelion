# Compline in the Seed

We look down the ribbed throat of the CUCULUS. The seed's armoured hull is a tunnel of heavy rings, scorched where it burrowed into the outpost. At its heart the fabricator's mouth is open and glowing. Inside it a print gantry hangs over a bed where a half-made limb lies in the heat, and the light spills out onto the grated deck. Three spare frames hang from a rail at the left edge, shadowed, the next bodies waiting their turn. The Commando's current frame sits on an ammunition crate in front of the mouth. His helmet is outlined against the glow, his head is bowed, and the rosary falls between his knees. A cable runs from the back of his neck up across the ribs to a small archive port with four status lights: two and a half thousand years of history going in while he prays. On the right, where the deck meets the hull, the mind has made a corner sacred: a cross cut from hull scrap, a crimson cloth over a block, and a candle-LED whose light catches the cross's near edges. Compline: the last hour, the furnace banked, warm and close.

How it was painted: on a dark warm ground. The hull went down in three tones that warm toward the mouth, blended at the seams, with the ribs laid over it as dark rings. Then came the hot throat, from orange at the rim to near-white low on the bed. The figure, rack and altar were laid in as opaque darks, dried, and repainted, because the first pass over the still-wet glow went translucent. The deck was the trouble. A soft-edged light pool dried as a hard oval, and blending it dragged the altar cloth into the floor. I repainted the whole deck in one session as a lit far plate and a dark near band, then repainted the altar. Edge lights from the mouth, the rivets, the scorch drags and the heat stipple all went on dry paint. One blend softened the scorches into the hull and turned the stipple into haze. Still weak: the platform reads a little like a stage, the cast shadow under the frame is a blob, and the rib rings are too regular. With more time I would break the ribs with plates and damage and give the frame's armour more modelling.
