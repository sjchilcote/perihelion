**Session: `first_death`** — **The First Death** (story beat).

"Die at least once (the first revival is a scripted-adjacent moment: the
new frame walks past the old frame's wreck…)". Before that: the death
itself. Paint the moment the first frame falls in the dark outpost — not
the gore, the stillness right after: the frame lying on a grated deck in a
MIRIAD corridor, its crimson tabard spread, one gauntlet still holding the
rosary, its optic going dark (one last cold point of light), spent casings
around it, the thing that killed it already leaving (only its shadow on the
wall, long, too many joints — a subverted outpost security machine), a
hazard lamp's slow amber pulse the only light. Far away in the dark, very
faint, the seed's warm glow: the fabricator already printing the next one.
Quiet, cold, a little holy. Landscape. Learn from tonight: value structure
first (almost all dark), lean darks, lights last on dry paint. Deadline 02:35.
