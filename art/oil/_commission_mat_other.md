Read the rules below first; they override anything above about composition.

the station: "inconsistency itself", "the walls breathe", cold inverted colour
(the Breach painting used pale-for-dark, cold-for-warm). The game warps these
floors at runtime, so this tile need not be crisp — but it must still tile.
Paint the Testator bronze floor (the same 125-unit course grid and bone inlay
as /tmp/cp/mat_bronze/final.png — look at it) being UNFOLDED: the inlay grid
still continues across the wrap, but plates are inverted (pale bodies, dark
lips), some cut by straight cold lines, some displaced or turned inside their
cells, colours gone cold (indigo, violet-black, sickly cyan, bone gone blue).
Keep values mid-dark. It is a deadline piece: aim for 20–30 minutes.
