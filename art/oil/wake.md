# One Wakes

Seconds after The Dormitory, in the same gallery and from the same viewpoint, the nearest Warden on the right has woken. Its eye blazes white-cold in the middle of its alcove. Its folded shell has split open into a crown of dark blade-petals with a crest rising out of the top, and two long jointed forelimbs are unfolding out of the alcove toward the aisle. Each blade and limb is edged cold on the side toward the eye. The eye's light floods the alcove in pale blue and spills across the floor, and it throws the Commando's long shadow back up the aisle behind him. All the other eye-points in both rows have brightened by one step. The Commando has turned to face it, half-crouched, with his cannon coming up. His lamp is now full on, and its narrow warm beam crosses the aisle to the Warden. Far off, the amber hazard lamp still burns on the end wall.

I rebuilt the gallery by re-running my own Dormitory chunks with the same coordinates, leaving the nearest right-hand alcove empty for the waking Warden. Then I added the one cold light source. The spill went down as short strokes in three blue-dark piles, chosen by distance from the eye. The Warden's petals, limbs and legs went down as dark polygons in that alcove's own coordinates, and the eye's glow was built from short radial strokes, blended along the radius only, all on dry paint. Again the paint surprised me. My first lamp beam was a thin glaze over a dark wall, and it dried into a speckled crackle. I painted the area back to wall and alcove and rebuilt the beam as a narrow wedge of dithered warm strokes, brightest at the lamp. Still short: the Commando is small and a little stiff, with a stray rim line across his body. The beam reads a little like a solid bar.
