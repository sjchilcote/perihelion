# The Hours, Kept in a Borrowed Chapel

A round Testator chamber deep in the TESTAMENT: bronze ribs rising into a dome
gone dark, a frieze of bone fold-notation glyphs (each a polygon holding a
smaller copy of itself turned by the golden angle), concentric inlay rings at
golden-ratio gaps around an off-centre dais. The MIRIAD crew dragged their gray
modular benches in here — identical legs at identical spacing — because the
room awed them. Now one shaft of sunlight falls from a nested heptagonal
oculus, and the Commando's frame kneels in it, back to us, praying the hours.
On the dais, a cross made from two scraps of outpost metal.

How it was painted: ~48 chunks, by the studio's director, alongside the six
commissioned painters. Mostly a record of learning the paint: unclipped broad
strokes overrun a mask by their own length; a dark glaze laid over a darker
passage comes out *lighter*; a violet glaze meant to neutralise a too-yellow
shaft turned it magenta; the light shaft was painted out and repainted three
times before a low-load blended lead-white veil, warmed with Mars orange, read
as light in air. Rim lights laid into wet bone black dissolve; they stuck after
six days' drying.
