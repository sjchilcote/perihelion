**Session: `horror`** — **What the frame became** (late-game concept, leaning into horror).

The Commando's frame changes radically over the game: it starts as the apex of
post-human combat technology — a roughly human-shaped Church soldier — and by
the end it is something beyond our comprehension: **a centauroid frame
bristling with weapons, manipulators and sensor masts.** On the apostasy path,
Adversary organs are grafted into it, until "the station's docents classify
him as what he hunts." Paint the late frame as horror: the thing that is now
walking the halls of the TESTAMENT. Think about what makes it frightening —
scale against the architecture, too many limbs doing too many things, sensor
masts like antlers or spines, something wrong where Adversary tissue meets
machined war-metal (the Adversary is inconsistency itself; nothing on it holds
still). Perhaps a small vestige of what he was — a scrap of crimson, a cross,
a rosary — makes it worse. Who sees it, and from where, is your decision (a
Testator docent's point of view? a corridor it fills? the light of something
it is doing?). Format your choice.
