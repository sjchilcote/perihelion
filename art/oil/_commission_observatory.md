**Session: `observatory`** — **The Observatory** (place concept).

The Testators watched the sky for a million years waiting for someone to
come. Paint their observatory on the TESTAMENT: a hemispherical bronze
dome whose inner surface is an orrery-map of every star they tracked (bone
studs in golden-angle spirals, inscribed circles in fold notation), a slot
in the dome open onto real space showing the Sun's edge as a hard white
arc, a great bronze telescope (or mirror array) in the centre pointed
through it, its cradle turning on a rail ring; in its eyepiece-chair, a
Testator docent, a million years dead or not dead, still watching. The
Commando entering at a low doorway, tiny. Warm bronze, cold starlight, one
hot white slot. Value structure first, lean darks, lights last. Portrait
or landscape. Deadline 03:05.
