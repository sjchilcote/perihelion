**Session: `kiln`** — **The Fabricator** (place concept).

The seed's fabricator — where the Commando's frames are printed. Paint it
from inside the print chamber: a deep round kiln-like space of scorched
armour ribs, a gantry overhead carrying a print head that glows white-hot,
a half-printed frame lying on the print bed (legs and pelvis complete,
torso a lattice of glowing lines, the rest still a cloud of sparks and
dust), feeders and hoses of raw material snaking in from the walls, the
heat light orange-white at the centre falling off to deep red and black at
the edges. Industrial, sacred, a womb. Value structure first (dark edges,
one blazing centre), lean darks, lights last on dry paint. Any format.
Deadline 03:15.
