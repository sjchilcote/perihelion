**Session: `library`** — **The Records Stack** (place concept).

From the game's README: "the records stack has no end." RECURSION STACK is
"the infinite corridor" — a Testator archive where the Testators kept the
records of everything they learned about the Adversary and about the
civilizations they hoped to warn, inscribed on bone tablets in fold
notation. Paint the stack from inside: tall shelves of bone tablets and
bronze cases rising out of sight above and below a narrow walkway, the
aisle running ahead into infinity (every so often the same reading-chair
and lamp, the same bronze docent at its desk, smaller each time), the
Commando reading one tablet by its light. Warm lamplight on bone, cool dark
between. Quiet, scholarly, infinite. Format your choice. Deadline 02:25.
