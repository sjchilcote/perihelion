# The Cartographer

A round bronze map-room in near-dark. Around its curved wall the shelves are packed with rolled plans, seen end-on as rows of pale discs. One lamp hangs on a chain over the middle, a bronze shade with a white bulb, and lights a huge circular drafting table of pale bone. The table is brightest at its centre and falls off in rings toward its bronze rim. Inked on it is a plan of the TESTAMENT written in fold notation. A hexagonal heart is ringed by six petal-rooms, each holding its turned inset (a smaller copy rotated by its loop's twist). The rooms are joined by bridging arcs with edge ticks and small arrows. A self-gluing room sits at the left, its arc leaving and returning to itself, and a long corridor-glyph at the right spirals inward through its insets. The docent leans over the far side, hooded, its bone face and single lens bent to the work, its sleeves lit along their tops. Its left hand sets a bronze compass on the plan, and its right holds a bone stylus over a corridor that has moved: the old line between the heart and a petal has been scraped pale, and a fresh dark line runs beside it. At the near right the Commando leans in at the table's edge, his gauntlet pointing at one of the petal-rooms, asking. The lamp lights the tops of his helm, pauldrons and pointing arm.

**How I painted it.** The light structure went in first: dark wall and floor, a soft warm ring on the wall behind, the table in three blended rings of bone. The plan was inked as foreshortened polygons. Polylines are smoothed into blobs when stroked, so I restated the room-glyphs edge by edge to get corners. The shelf pass with one small work() per roll took seven minutes, so later rolls would be single brush touches. The docent laid into wet paint came out translucent and was restated opaque. The Commando's rims laid four days after his body vanished, and they held only after eight days. Still short: the docent's face reads as a flat bone mask stuck to a ball, the lamp's halo is grainy stipple, the Commando is a flat dark mannequin with little modelling, and the wall shelves have no visible boards.
