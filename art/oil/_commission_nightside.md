**Session: `nightside`** — **The Night Side** (exterior concept).

The TESTAMENT's night side, away from the Sun: the hull's worn bronze scale-
plates barely visible, lit only by the ring's thousands of windows (a few
warm, most dark — the station keeps its lamps for guests), the faint blue of
starlight on plate edges, the Sun's corona glowing around the hull's limb
as a thin burning edge behind the station — and on that dark hull, very
small, a single lit thing: the dead MIRIAD outpost's last working beacon
blinking amber, or the Commando's helmet lamp moving along the hull. The
scale should be immense and the light almost nothing. Learn from tonight:
decide the value structure first (almost all dark), keep the darks lean
(no white in them), lay the lights last on dry paint. Landscape.
Deadline 02:10.
