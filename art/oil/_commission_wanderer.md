**Session: `wanderer`** — **The Wanderer above the Sun** (concept art).

An homage to Caspar David Friedrich's *Wanderer above the Sea of Fog*, in this
world: the Commando's frame — a roughly human-shaped walking robot, a soldier
of the Church — stands on the outer hull of the TESTAMENT, seen from behind
(a Rückenfigur), looking out. Below and beyond him the hull's bronze scale-
plates curve away in golden-angle spirals; beyond them, the Sun, near and vast,
and the black. Whatever he is looking at, we look at it with him. Friedrich
never painted a face in these pictures; you shouldn't either. Portrait format
(Friedrich's Wanderer is about 0.79 wide to 1 tall). The mood: a pilgrim at the
edge of the sublime, the stillness of prayer, a weapon that knows it is a
weapon.
