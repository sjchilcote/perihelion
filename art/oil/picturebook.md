# Ratio and the Big Knight

A picture-book double spread in flat, soft shapes and a warm limited palette, with a gutter down the middle and a line of abstract text under each page.

On the left page, under a warm peach sky, a low ochre-green hill holds a CRT-shaped mound with a pale blue-green screen. On top of it stands Ratio: tiny, black hair, white dress, waving up with two little motion marks by her hand. Filling the right half of the page an enormous, kind, simplified knight is kneeling down to see her. He has a big round grey helm with a dark visor band and one warm dot of light for an eye, a crimson tabard with a pale cross, and one big arm reaching down until his hand rests at the edge of her hill.

On the right page the sky is black with a few small stars, there is one big round warm sun, and the curve of a great gold ring runs across the bottom. Far along it the two of them walk together, small: the knight in his crimson tabard reaching down, and Ratio a few steps beside him, holding his hand.

How it was painted: page grounds and the gutter first, as large flat fills; then the hill, the screen, the sun, the ring and the knight's big shapes; then the tabard, the cross, the visor and Ratio; then the eyes, cheeks, motion marks and text lines. The night page taught the usual lesson again. Yellow laid onto the wet black came out mottled olive, and the sun and ring had to dry and be repainted. Ratio's face and dress greyed where they met her wet hair, and the knight's eye-light went greenish, so all three were repainted on dry paint. Still weak: the knight's grey is a little cold for the warm palette, and the walking pair on the right page is small enough that Ratio nearly vanishes.
