**Session: `epilogue`** — **Both Factions Look Up** (ending epilogue).

The Faith + Trust ending's epilogue: "the alliance forming; the war outside
going quiet as both factions look up." Twenty-five centuries after the
machine war for the memory of humanity, the solar system is still split
between machine factions — MIRIAD (human-derived utility minds: right
angles, grids, cool gray and amber, industrial signage) and the loyalist
Church (a post-human Catholic war-faction run from linked supercomputer-
bunkers: crosses, crimson, bone). Paint the moment the war goes quiet: in
the inner solar system, two fleets that were about to engage — MIRIAD's
gray-and-amber ships and the Church's crimson-and-bone ones — hanging
motionless facing each other across a gap, their weapons cold, every one of
them turned toward the same distant point of light: the TESTAMENT, its new
solar sail spread and catching the Sun, the two witnesses' testimony
broadcast from it. Vast, still, hopeful. The ships small; the space huge.
Landscape. Deadline 01:15.
