**Session: `friedrich`** — **Monk by the Sea** (a Friedrich study).

A fourth master for you: Caspar David Friedrich's "The Monk by the Sea"
(1808–10) — a tiny figure on a pale strip of shore, a dark band of sea,
and an enormous, nearly empty sky occupying four-fifths of the canvas;
radical emptiness, horizontal bands, no repoussoir. Subject: the
Commando, tiny, standing on the TESTAMENT's outer hull (a thin pale band
of bronze scale-plates across the bottom fifth), before a dark band of
deep space, and above it an immense sky — the Sun's corona filling it
from below as a vast gradient of pale gold rising into cold blue-black,
nothing else. The figure no taller than a fingernail, back to us, crimson
rag the only saturated mark. Broad horizontal bands, wet blended
gradients, the figure last on dry paint. 6–15 chunks. Landscape (wide).
