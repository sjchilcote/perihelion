**Session: `lattice`** — **The Lattice, from the Other Side** (concept).

The Adversary "experience embedded, Euclidean-ish space the way we would
experience a crystal lattice: rigid, sterile, offensive". Paint ordinary
space as THEY see it: the TESTAMENT (or a corridor of it) rendered as an
unbearable rigid lattice — every plate, rib and window locked into hard,
cold, repeating geometry, glaring, airless, a prison of right angles and
exact arcs — seen from outside it, from a space that is soft, folded,
flowing, warm-dark and alive (their home: no edges, no consistent
geometry, everything folding into more of itself), with a few of their
organs pressing against the lattice from outside and one place where a seam
has opened and the folding is pouring in. The emotional inversion is the
point: for once the geometry is the horror and the dissolving is the
relief. Format your choice. Deadline 02:05.
