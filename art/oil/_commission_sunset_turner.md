**Session: `sunset_turner`** — **The Fall of the Testament** (a second Turner study).

Your "Perihelion" (/tmp/cp/sunrise/final.png) worked beautifully in the
manner of late Turner. A companion piece in the same handling: this time
in the register of Turner's "Slave Ship" or "Snow Storm — Steam-Boat off a
Harbour's Mouth" — violence instead of serenity: the moment the Adversary
comes through in force (the station's interior lights going out in waves,
the cold violet of the Breach tearing through the gold), a storm of warm and
cold colour in sweeping curves, the station a dark broken shape caught in
the vortex, a few hard notes to hold it. Same rules: pale ground, big wet
glazes and scumbles blended in sweeping curves, a few crisp darks/lights at
the end on dry paint; free strokes along the vortex rather than masked
passes. 15–25 chunks. Landscape. Deadline 02:20.
