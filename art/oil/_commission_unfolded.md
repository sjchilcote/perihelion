**Session: `unfolded`** — **The Unfolded** (concept art for an ending: the final shot).

Apostasy + Betrayal: "Faith gone, Ratio dead, rules silent, frame and mind
rebuilt around Adversary organs until the station's docents classify him as
what he hunts. He carries the war through the breach and exterminates
everything on the far side — the weapon system functioning perfectly, aimed
by nothing — then returns to a solar system still tearing itself apart over
how best to love a dead species, and extinguishes the last embers of the
machine civil war, faction by faction, archive by archive, ending with the
WMC's mountain and, finally, with himself. The cuckoo, at last, empties
every nest. (Presentation note: this ending is horror, framed entirely as
loss — the game's answer to 'what is this protagonist for?' shown by
subtraction. No power-fantasy grading, no triumphal score; **the final shot
is the empty vault.**)"

Paint the final shot: **the empty vault.** The vault where humanity's
seed-bank (the Ark) was kept — Testator bronze and bone, a cradle or plinth
made for a small human container — now empty. Subtraction: what is absent
must be felt. Perhaps traces: frost where the container lay, a scrap of
crimson cloth, a rosary dropped, the marks of too many legs in the dust, a
dead terminal where Ratio once lit up, the station's lights going out one
row at a time. No figure (or only the remains of one). Quiet, cold, total.
Format your choice.
