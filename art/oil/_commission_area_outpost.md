**Session: `area_outpost`** — **Establishing shot: the Outpost** (visual-language concept for a game area).

A scene-setting shot of the first stratum, the MIRIAD research outpost (DOCK 4,
COOLANT ROW, HYDROPONICS TORUS): hasty prefab, bolted to the outside of
something much older. Right angles, grids, modular identical parts, cool
structural grays and amber hazard; machine-language placards; wear that says
*refusal* (patched, jury-rigged, mismatched replacement parts, hand-painted
supplements to printed signage). It has gone dark and airless; its own
security machines hunt its halls. It is almost entirely Euclidean — but only
almost: the storage ring holds nine segments in an eight-segment footprint; a
pump-room hatch drops you into a different pump room facing the other way;
walk half the hydroponics torus and you have walked a full loop. The
geometry must not announce itself; at most one detail, for the attentive eye,
is a little wrong. The job of this painting is to define the area's visual
language for the team: materials, light, palette, scale, mood. Format your
choice (landscape suits an establishing shot).
