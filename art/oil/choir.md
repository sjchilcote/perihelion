# The Choir of Docents

We look down the length of a tall bronze choir. Five tiers of stalls step up and back on either side and run away in perspective to a far bronze wall. Every stall holds a docent: 120 of them, bell-shaped bronze robes with bone faces, all turned toward the aisle and the far end. At the far end a bone lectern stands on a low bronze dais. On it an open tablet of fold notation, nested turned pentagons on each leaf, blazes white in the one pale shaft of light that falls on it from an oculus high in the dark vault. Each docent's face carries a single cold lens catching that light, a hundred small cold points along the tiers, and the shoulder of each robe toward the lectern takes a thin warm edge. Above, bronze pilasters and transverse ribs fade into near-black. Just inside the near end, left of the aisle's centre, the Commando has come in and stopped: small and dark, with a faint cool edge from the shaft on his helmet and shoulders.

**How I painted it.** I built a one-point perspective with five tiers per side (tread and riser masks) and laid the value structure in lean darks: a near-black hall, dark bronze stalls with treads a step lighter than their risers, a dark aisle and far wall. One helper painted each docent far to near as two filbert strokes for the robe, a touch for the head and a short bone stroke for the face on the lectern side. Lights went in last on set paint. What went wrong: the first shaft, a lead-white glaze, came out as an opaque white bar, and so did a stray window. I rebuilt the shaft as three opaque graded bands of air, only a little lighter than the dark, with blended seams. The first Commando was far too big and stood right in front of the lectern. I painted him out region by region and put him back smaller and off the axis. The docents' shoulder strokes, laid unclipped, ran across him and had to be painted over. Still short: the faces are soft blobs rather than modelled bone masks, the shoulder strokes read a little stripey, a faint smudge of the old window remains top left, and his rim light is very faint.
