**Session: `ark`** — **The Ark in the remass bay** (concept art for the game's emotional core).

Deep in the TESTAMENT's spine, in a landing bay among the reaction-mass tanks
where the station grinds down ingested rock and ice, rests a small, unnatural,
human-made object the station recognized and *kept*: humanity's seed-bank, a
sample container that drifted out of the Solar system on a randomized
trajectory, flagged by the Testators *preserve at all costs*. It is the thing
everyone came for. It is small, old, frost-rimed, human — tiny in a vast,
dim, handmade bronze cavern heaped with ground rock. The Commando's frame
stands or kneels at it, reading its diagnostics. (What they say — the embryos
are almost certainly long dead — is the game's great sorrow and the test of his
hope; the picture should be able to hold both.) Light is your instrument here:
the one warm light, or the one cold one. Format your choice.
