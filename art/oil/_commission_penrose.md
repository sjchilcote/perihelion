**Session: `penrose`** — **The Endless Stair** (level concept; your last).

A bronze stair that climbs forever and returns to where it began — the
Penrose stair (Escher's "Ascending and Descending"), built into the
Gauntlet: seen from above at an angle, a square of four flights of
bronze steps around an open light-well, each flight climbing, the four
together closing into a loop; small Commando figures (the same one,
four times) climbing each flight in turn; lamps at each landing; the
light-well below glowing violet. Your method: lay the four flights in
careful perspective first (isometric-ish, so the impossibility holds),
then steps, rails, figures and lamps as strokes on dry paint. Square or
4:3. Deadline 06:20.
