**Session: `tabard`** — **Taking Up the Tabard** (story beat; closes the trilogy).

Your First Death and Second Frame make a pair; close it. Same corridor,
same lamp, same coordinates. Later: the wreck is gone (hauled to the
seed for parts — only a scorch outline on the grating and a few spent
casings remain). The new frame stands, back half-turned to us, pulling
the old crimson tabard over its shoulders — the torn, scorched one, the
bone cross on it broken and mended with wire — the rosary already wound
on its left gauntlet. It is going on down the corridor. The amber lamp,
the seed's warm glow far off. Quiet, resolved. Learn from the last two:
draw the figure as plates from the start (helm, gorget, pauldrons,
breastplate, faulds, cuisses, greaves), not tubes; crimson on dry paint.
