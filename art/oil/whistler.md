# Nocturne: the Seed in the Dark

A Whistler nocturne in blue and gold. The sky is one long wash of close grey-blues and blue-greens, mottled like river mist and a shade paler toward the bottom. Low across the lower third lies the seed, the Commando's burrowed pod, as a long dark shape, and its hatch is a single point of warm gold with a faint glow around it. Far above, the TESTAMENT is a vast pale blue-grey arch, like Old Battersea Bridge seen through night fog. A darker band runs along its underside, and a sparse, uneven scatter of tiny gold sparks marks distant windows. There is almost no detail, and the picture is quiet, flat and decorative.

How it was painted: 5 chunks, 2:1, on a mid blue-grey knifed ground. I laid thin, lean washes in long ruler-straight horizontal strokes and blended everything wet. The arch went in as free filbert strokes along its curve and the seed as long low strokes, all wet. My first layer was too pale for a nocturne, and a second deeper wash over everything, blended, buried the arch completely. I restated it wet-in-wet with a darker inner band and a clean badger dragged along its curve. The gold points went on last over dry paint. The first set was too small to read at full view and had to be restated larger. With more time I would make the arch thinner and more ghostly, since it reads a little heavy and tubular, and give the sky Whistler's smoother, more liquid surface instead of this mottling.
