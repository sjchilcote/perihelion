# Dock 4 — Sweep

The first place the player walks: the MIRIAD outpost's storage ring, gone dark.
We look along the ring corridor. Prefab ribs frame each identical section. On
the left wall are shelving racks of cargo pods, and the right wall is a row of
numbered bay doors under amber-and-black hazard bands. Because this is a ring,
the floor rises ahead and slides out of sight under the ceiling, so the
corridor bends away into darkness. In the foreground on the left is a docking
collar with a dead round hatch inside a striped ring. Machine-language
placards are stuck on the ribs. Nothing is lit except a few failing emergency
strips at the foot of the walls (two amber, one red, the rest dead) and the
Commando's helmet lamp. The Commando is small, mid-distance, seen from
behind, walking the ring with his lamp throwing a cold oval ahead of him onto
the rising floor. The doors count 7, 8, 9 as you go away from us, and then
the lamp lights the next door: 1. Further on there is a 2, almost lost in the
dark. An attentive player will count too many bays for the curve.

## How it was painted

I made it in 13 chunks on a dark cool-grey ground at 16:9. I set the
corridor up in perspective with the ring's curvature built in (the floor
rises as depth squared over twice the ring radius). Then I painted each
surface far to near, slice by slice, with separate piles, so nearer paint
covered farther paint, and laid the ribs, doors and racks the same way. The
lamp light is three graded piles on the floor, fused with a badger, plus a
low-load glaze for the beam in the air. Several things went wrong first:
- My first build was far too light and evenly grey, a tunnel with no dread,
  so I scumbled and repainted the near walls and floor dark.
- The stencilled numerals came out back to front, because I had mapped
  "forward along the wall" the wrong way. I painted them out and stencilled
  them again.
- The emergency-strip glows came out as harsh orange smears, so I repainted
  them as narrow warm bands.
- A blender pass over the hatch's shadow erased its hub, so I repainted the
  hatch in three graded piles and put the hub and bolts back on dry paint.
Still rough: the beam streaks at the far end are scratchy, and the near
walls are mostly flat dark. The lit "1" is only a foreshortened bar. I would
want more incident on the near left wall, and a cleaner, softer lamp
pool.

## Pass at 01:18 CDT, 2 Oct 2026

What changed (chunks 14–25):
- **The ring curves.** I repainted everything inside the near frame with a
  lateral bend. Past the frame, the corridor's centreline swings left as
  (z−4.7)²/18. The outer wall, with its bay doors, now sweeps across the view
  toward the left, the ribs and doors foreshorten around the bend, and the far
  end disappears behind the inner wall's edge.
- **Stencils.** The bay numbers are blocky MIRIAD stencils with stencil
  bridges, in amber on grey: a large 7 on the near door, then 8 and 9 going
  away. Around the bend, on the door his lamp lights, is a clear white-gold
  **1**. My first 7 and 8 went olive over wet door paint, so I laid them
  again on dry paint.
- **Commando.** He stands to the left of the lit door now, lamp turned
  toward it, with a cold pool ahead of him. A red strip still burns at the
  foot of the inner wall.
- **Floor.** The near deck has converging plate seams, grime and warm light
  from the amber strip raking along the seam edges.
- **Collar.** A heavier flange with 20 bolts and a lit lip.

What still falls short: I first painted him standing right in front of the
door he lights, and painting him out took three tries. The last one left a
pale box at the foot of door 1. It can pass for a crate, but it wasn't
planned. The beam has scratchy white dashes left of him. The near floor is
still mostly flat dark, and the raking light is thin. The 9 is legible but
narrow.
