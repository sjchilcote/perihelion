# Ratio and the Big Knight: endpapers

The endpapers of the picture book: a double page of cream scattered with the book's small characters in a friendly diagonal repeat. Each diagonal carries one motif:
- little knights in crimson tabards with pale crosses, each waving, with two tiny motion marks by his hand;
- tiny black-haired Ratios in white dresses, smiling and waving;
- sleepy purple bugs curled up with blue spikes, eyes shut, and a little violet Z;
- small orange suns with pointed rays;
- little gold rings;
- clusters of gold four-point stars.

Small peach dots sit in the gaps between motifs. The colours are the book's own: crimson, cream, warm grey, violet, orange and gold.

How it was painted: each motif was drawn by one helper per kind, placed on a staggered grid so that the same kind falls along each diagonal. Every colour area was laid directly on the bare cream ground, side by side, never over another wet colour. All the shapes of one colour across the whole page went down in a single pass, which kept the chunks short. After drying, the knights got a deeper grey so they stand off the cream, and the eyes, smiles, Zs and dots went on. A faint gutter fold I stroked down the middle crossed the centre Ratio's face and one ring, so it was painted out again, and that Ratio and ring were restated. Still weak: the motifs are all the same size and upright, so the repeat is a little regular, and the knights' grey is still close to the ground in value.
