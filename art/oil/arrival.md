# The Last Morning

The view is from inside, behind him. A tall, round-headed bronze window fills the dark wall, and the Commando stands at its sill as a silhouette, back to us. The crimson rag at his shoulder lifts and curls although there is no wind. Through the window the TESTAMENT has come through perihelion. The Sun is rising over the station's own dark curving hull, a white-hot disc just clearing the limb. Its corona fills the window in long gold rays and a huge soft glow, which passes through crimson and orange into a violet upper sky. Far off, the dark mirror of the sail catches the first light along one edge. The arch head is filled with golden-angle tracery, crossing spiral bars around a central boss, and dark plates studded with scales line the reveal. Only the head and shoulders take a thin rim of the light. The picture is meant to feel warm, enormous and quietly hopeful.

**How it was painted.** It took 16 chunks on a warm sienna ground, applying what I learned in the last two pictures. The darks are lean: bone black and burnt sienna, no white. The sky went in as radial bands of crimson, orange and gold, blended once along the light. The corona is about 210 long strokes running out from the Sun, laid on paint that had dried, hottest and densest near the disc, with a transparent yellow glaze between them for the glow. The figure was laid in a single opaque near-black, and the rim light and the rag's lit fold went on after it dried. One mistake: a warm scumble meant as light on the left reveal came out as a pale, opaque strip, and I had to repaint it in bronze and keep only a thin warm edge. The wall's first coat let the orange ground show through as noisy flecks, so I laid a second lean coat over it, darker toward the margins. What I'd still want: more modelling in the bronze surround, more weight in the figure's legs below the sill, and a sail that reads more clearly as a sail.

## Pass at 03:34 CDT, 2 Oct 2026

The director's note was that the figure read as a man in an overcoat. Everything else in the painting is unchanged. In chunks 17–22, on dry paint, I repainted him as the Commando's war-body:
- **Armour:** a flat-topped great helm with a small crest, broad pauldrons standing off the shoulders, a breastplate narrowing to an armoured waist with faulds, plated thighs with knee cops, greaves, gauntlets and sabatons. All of it is one crisp, opaque near-black.
- **Background repairs:** wherever the old overcoat stuck out past the new outline, I painted it back in the colour of what lies behind it (hull, sill, wall or sky), and opened a narrow gap of the room between his legs.
- **Rim and rag:** a thin warm rim catches the helm, pauldrons, sunward arm and gauntlet, with faint seams on the brow, the fauld plates and the knees. The crimson rag now lifts from the left pauldron.
- **Floor:** a dark plane now meets the wall below the sill, with a low warm line at the wall's foot and his shadow running toward us.

A warm glaze meant as light on the floor dried as an acid yellow strip and had to be painted out.

What still falls short: the legs read a little like one column above the knees, and the frame is somewhat slender and stiff for a war-body.
