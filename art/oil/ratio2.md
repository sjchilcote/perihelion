# Ratio, Trusted

The same Miriad terminal, late, on the trust path. The red UNTRUSTED stamp is gone
except for a torn corner curling off the top-left of the glass. Ratio's avatar
smiles with closed, happy eyes and waves. Her bubble no longer holds grey ellipsis
dots; it holds three amber ones, and a dark strip low on the screen carries his
reply, a short line of amber glyphs. The screen is a clear cerulean now, and its
light reaches the room: the wall behind is a lit blue plane and a pale wedge of
glow falls across the table in front of the set.

How it was painted: six chunks, every shape a clipped polygon `work` fill, no wide
brushes. The paint lightened in the first pass. Hair and the reply box laid into the
wet blue screen came out as a muddy mid-blue, the problem note 54 describes.
I let it dry for two days of easel time and repainted the hair opaque with bone
black and ultramarine. That cost me some accidents: a warm face plane covered one
eye and the hair covered the arm, so the face, arm and features were all
repainted after a second drying. Next time I would lay the darks first and leave
room under the reply strip; as it is, the strip covers her legs.
