# Beneath the Wheel

A second title screen. We are far below the plane of the TESTAMENT, looking up. The Sun fills the top of the picture, a white-hot disc whose lower limb burns through orange and dull red into a violet-black sky. Across it, the habitat ring is seen from underneath as a huge dark wheel: rim, seven spokes and hub in silhouette against the glare. The spine comes down out of the hub toward us and leaves the frame at the lower left, dark and immense. There is no air, so there are no beams. The light that comes down through the gaps between the spokes falls on the spine's underside in four hard slices of bronze. In each slice a row of vault bays shows as dark recesses with lit lips. Near the cropped end a few faint rims hint at an ingestion maw. Everything else is unlit. In the empty dark at the lower right, a single tiny white-hot spark trails a thin line across the sky: the CUCULUS. The lower third is kept dark and quiet for the logo.

I settled the values first. The toned dark violet ground serves as the sky. The Sun went in as three opaque zones, and the radiance below its limb as short radial strokes in five piles, chosen by distance from the limb with a little scatter and blended along the radius only. The station is one lean opaque silhouette pass with no white, laid on dry paint. The slices of light and every rim, bay lip and the spark went on later, also on dry paint. One mistake: rim lines I drew along the ring and spokes ran across the nearer spine, because I never clipped them to the parts of the station they belong to. Painting them out took three tries. The first two left beaded ghosts. The third covered them with a dense detail pass over a narrow ribbon mask, in dark on the shadowed spine and bronze inside the lit slices. Next time I would clip every rim to its own part from the start. I would also give the maw a real, legible form instead of three arcs.

## Pass at 01:38 CDT, 2 October

Chunks 10–15. The aim was to replace the wagon wheel with the TESTAMENT's own forms and scale.
- **Ring.** The ring is now a massive dark band, about 40–45 units deep, with the spine kept in front of it. On dry paint I added:
  - six rows of faint bronze scale-plate crescents on the underside, with warm lips on the far arc where the glare grazes it;
  - two rows of tiny windows along the inner face, mostly dark, with a few warm runs restated larger;
  - a thin hot line on the inner edge, where the ring faces up into the light.
- **Spokes.** They are thicker and taper from hub to rim, which is enlarged. Each spoke carries two rails, a truss zigzag, a pipe along one side and a warm rim line along its upper edge.
- **Spine.** Each lit slice now holds offset rows of scale-plates, each a dark crescent with a lit lip, set between the vault bays. The flat stripes are gone.
- **Ingestion maw.** The spine now ends in it. I cleared the spine back to sky beyond the mouth. The maw is a flared, ribbed bell, dark inside, with radial ribs on the flare, receding rib rings lit on their sunward arcs, and a hot upper lip.
- **Lower third.** It stays dark, apart from the CUCULUS spark.

Still short:
- The maw reaches down into the logo third on the far left.
- Its inner ribs are faint, so the bell reads more by its outline than by its depth.
- The ring's plates read as an even texture rather than as individual handmade plates.
- The light slices on the spine are still hard-edged geometric bands.
